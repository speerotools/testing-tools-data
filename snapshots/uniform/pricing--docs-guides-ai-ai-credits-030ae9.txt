AI credits
AI credits are required to use AI-powered features in Uniform, including
Scout
,
AI Quick edits
, and
MCP Server
create/search operations. Understanding how credits work helps you manage your team's AI usage effectively.
Overview
#
AI credits are allocated monthly at the team level and shared across all projects and users within your team. The allocation is based on the team's active production projects or the credits defined in your team's subscription.
How AI credits work
#
AI Credit allocation
#
Credits are assigned monthly
to your team and expire at the end of each month—unused credits do not carry over.
When your team's credits are exhausted, AI capabilities will be disabled
until the next monthly allocation or a
manual top-up
.
Monitoring your balance
#
You can monitor your team's current monthly usage and remaining credits on your
team's subscription page
or on the
team dashboard
.
AI credits usage shown on team dashboard
Click the
icon next to the credit count to see when credits reset, open a usage breakdown, or request more credits.
The credits popover, with a link to the usage breakdown and an option to get more credits.
View usage breakdown
opens a monthly usage report. The chart shows up to six months of actual usage. When there is enough history, it also predicts usage for the remainder of the current month and next month. Select a past or current month to see credits used by user — including
automations
that consume AI credits — and by project.
Monthly AI credit usage broken down by user and by project. Automations appear alongside people in the user list.
Predicted usage
#
If there is sufficient usage history, the chart estimates how many credits your team is likely to use for the rest of this month and for next month:
Solid bars
are credits already used.
Hatched segments
are predicted usage.
Trend
shows a recency-weighted average of actual monthly usage. The legend indicates the trend direction.
Limit
is your team's current monthly allocation. It may not be visible in the chart, but you can hover over the legend to see your current allocation.
The user and project lists always show actual usage.
Predictions are for planning only
Predicted usage does not change your credit balance or monthly allocation. Estimates are based on recent usage and may be incorrect.
How predicted usage is calculated
Trendline:
A recency-weighted average of monthly totals.
Trend direction:
A linear regression of the trendline
Predictions:
The recency-weighted averages are applied to the number of days left in the current month and number of days in the next month.
Credit usage
#
Credits are consumed whenever you use Uniform features powered by AI, including:
Scout chats:
Chats with Scout use the most credits because Scout processes your project's context and Uniform concepts. To save credits, keep your chats short and focused. The amount of credits used depends on the length and complexity of the chat.
AI Quick edits:
Quick edits are made at the parameter level (for components) or field level (for entries), and usually use a small number of credits. More complex edits, like those involving advanced parameter types or larger changes, may use more credits.
MCP Server operations:
Create, edit, and search operations for compositions, entries, and patterns performed through the
MCP Server
require a similar amount of AI credits as content edits through AI Quick edits.
Automations that use AI:
A
Scout automation
run consumes credits at the same rate as a Scout chat, and so does a code automation that
calls Scout
. Because automations run unattended, use
trigger filters
so they only run when they are needed.
Out of credits?
When your team's credits are exhausted, AI capabilities will be disabled until the next monthly allocation or a manual top-up. Plan accordingly to maintain access to AI features.
Requesting additional credits
#
If needed, credits can be manually replenished by Uniform to ensure uninterrupted access to AI features. Reach out to your Account Representative from Uniform to request additional credits, or use this
form
.
Get more credits
in the credits popover on the team dashboard opens the same form.
