What is Uniform?
Uniform is the premier agentic digital experience platform businesses trust to orchestrate content and commerce for human customers and AI agents, solving today’s critical challenge of making brands discoverable and transactable through AI search engines.
Uniform is the original composable orchestration layer, uniting the entire technology stack through codeless connections and a Visual Workspace, where creators can access all content and data to design and publish digital experiences from a single tab. With cross-system agentic AI, advanced personalization, visual editing, and governance guardrails, Uniform helps digital teams reduce time to market and adopt AI while maintaining quality and compliance. Whether as the primary CMS or an experience layer on top of existing systems, Uniform helps you optimize your digital real estate for both human visitors and AI agents.
Explore the platform
Is Uniform a headless CMS?
Yes. Uniform is a best-in-class headless CMS with typed, structured content, APIs, and visual editing on any front-end, and it also orchestrates and integrates with existing CMSs, so teams can build natively in Uniform, keep the CMS they have, or run both together.
As a CMS, Uniform provides content types, entries, an asset library, localization, and in-context visual editing. As an orchestration layer, it composes content from Contentful, Sitecore, Contentstack, and many other systems alongside its own. Running both platforms simultaneously is a common pattern during a phased migration.
Explore the CMS
What is an agentic DXP?
An agentic DXP is a digital experience platform where AI agents take real action across the stack, executing tasks like building pages, translating content, and optimizing for answer engines, while humans set the outcomes and approve what ships.
The difference is execution. A traditional DXP waits for people to do the work. An agentic DXP exposes typed content and tools to agents so they can carry out multi step tasks, then routes the result back to a human for approval.
Does Uniform replace my existing CMS?
Only if you want it to. Uniform works both ways: use it as your headless CMS, or keep your current CMS and let Uniform orchestrate content, data, commerce, and AI on top of it, migrating gradually or not at all.
Most teams start by adding orchestration and agents on top of what they already run, then move content where it makes the most sense. A single full migration is never required, and the business controls the pace of change.

What is Scout?
Scout is Uniform's AI agent that takes projects from idea to live in hours, handling content creation, translation, GEO and AEO optimization, accessibility, and personalization from a natural-language prompt, with humans approving what ships.
Scout works inside the same typed content model as the rest of Uniform, so what it produces is structured, reviewable, and legible to answer engines rather than a wall of generated text.
Explore Scout
How is Scout different from AI assistants in other CMS platforms?
Scout executes real work across the entire Uniform platform rather than suggesting edits: teams request outcomes, Scout performs the tasks, and humans review and approve, so output scales with compute instead of headcount.
Assistants draft copy inside a text field. Scout builds and changes the experience itself, including pages, components, translations, personalization rules, and accessibility fixes. Every change stays reviewable before it ships.
How does Uniform improve AEO and GEO (answer engine and generative engine optimization)?
Uniform structures every piece of content as typed entries with explicit relationships and exposes it through MCP and API surfaces, so answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude can parse, cite, and act on it accurately.
Because content is typed rather than stored as page blobs, the relationships between products, claims, and pages stay explicit. This is what lets an answer engine attribute a statement to Uniform correctly instead of inferring it from rendered HTML.

Scout can stand up a complete experience in hours. Adding Uniform to an existing stack is typically a quarter of work, and gradual migration runs on your timeline. Pricing follows the path you choose.
