The visual workspace
One surface
for every source
The Uniform Visual Workspace is where digital teams compose, preview, and ship experiences built from every connected source: CMS content, commerce, customer data, search, and your own APIs, and where everything that ships, whether a human made it or Scout did, gets reviewed and approved.
The problem
You can not see the whole experience
Composable stacks made every capability best-of-breed and made the experience itself invisible: the hero lives in the CMS, the products in commerce, the offer logic in a third tool, and the only place they meet is a staging environment after a developer assembles them. Five tabs, three systems, one experience nobody can see whole until it is almost too late to change. The Visual Workspace is where the composed experience becomes visible, editable, and approvable in one place, before it ships.

FAQ
Questions about the Visual Workspace
What the Visual Workspace is, how it differs from a page builder and from the visual editing in your CMS, and how Scout's work gets reviewed in it.
What is a visual workspace?
A Visual Workspace is a surface where digital teams compose, preview, and manage experiences assembled from multiple sources (CMS content, commerce, data, search) as one visual whole. The Uniform Visual Workspace adds inline editing within developer-defined guardrails, workflows, and approvals, and review of AI-agent work before it ships.
What is a visual workspace?
How is a visual workspace different from a page builder?
Page builders generate their own pages from their own blocks, trapping content in layouts. The Uniform Visual Workspace composes real, managed front-end components from structured content from any source, so everything stays reusable, testable, and API-accessible: the visual layer edits the system; it doesn't replace it.
How is this different from the visual editing in my CMS?
CMS visual editors work only on that CMS's content. The Uniform Visual Workspace composes and edits across every connected source at once: your CMS, commerce, customer data, and APIs, in one canvas with one preview, which is the part single-repository editors structurally cannot do.
How does Scout use the visual workspace?
Scout's work lands in the Workspace as staged, previewable compositions: drafts, variants, translations, and bulk changes arrive as reviewable experiences, not chat suggestions. Your team inspects, edits, and approves before publishing, with autonomy dialed per task and every agent action logged and reversible.
Does composing visually lock my content into Uniform?
No. The Uniform Visual Workspace composes; it does not absorb. Content stays structured in its source systems (Uniform's CMS or the ones you already own) and remains fully API-accessible, so the same content serves other channels, other tools, and the AI agents that consume it.
