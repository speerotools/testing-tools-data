Visual Workspace
The only visual builder for managing composable digital experiences
Unblock creativity while optimizing your team’s productivity and time to market with Uniform Visual Workspace.
Unblock your team to visually manage multi-source digital experiences
For years brands have struggled to visually manage the digital experiences with their
headless CMSes
, in addition having a
composable digital experience stack
only makes it harder to manage experiences at scale.
Uniform is designed to bring any technology, data and content together with your design system of choice and make it simple and powerful to visually create and manage your digital experiences.
10 ways Uniform is built for Scale
In addition to our foundational abilities, which allow users to create and manage any composable digital experience, Uniform is built for scalability and optimized to increase speed to market—even for complex enterprise requirements.

Editions
Editions is a powerful feature that enables the creation and management of multiple temporary versions of pages or different structures for specific markets. Editions are designed to address the complex needs of global, multi-market digital strategies.
Localization
Uniform Localization
empowers marketers and merchandisers to create unique pages for every locale to deliver experiences with regional and linguistic accuracy. Clone pages for every market in seconds and optimize market-specific experiences in clicks.
Multisource Editing
Multi-source editing with Uniform lets users and web producers stay in the same tab to update content in connected sources and preview both draft and published content.
One of the big benefits of Uniform is that besides having content available through our edge APIs, your structured
headless content
can be connected with any front-end in clicks and managed visually.
Benefits & Features
Visually build for any channel
Uniform isn’t limited to websites. Manage your app, interactive kiosks, and other digital experiences visually from Uniform.
Powerful content management
Manage your content models in clicks and visually make edits to any content.
Scheduled publishing
Create and manage future releases and have everything from pages to components and content entries go live at the same time.
Inline editing
Select any component and manage the content inline - of course only the content that is allowed to be changed.
Workflow
Visually manage your different workflows and determine which roles can edit, approve, publish etc. You are in full control.
Ease of use
Any user can use Uniform to visually create and manage digital experiences. All controls can be adjusted from simple to advanced rights.
Sitemap
Don’t worry about the structure of your site and managing URL routes through developer backlog items; Uniform has a Sitemap with URLs and redirects included.
Control every detail
Great digital experiences need amazing copy and the perfect layout. Uniform puts the front-end control options back in the business teams’ hands by allowing as many components and variations as they need and the design team will support. That means marketers have more freedom, but always stay on brand, even when it comes to
personalization
.
Complete component control
By separating front-end components from the data powering them, Uniform Visual Workspace empowers Marketing teams to get creative and build pages faster. For example, a product carousel can easily be remapped to show speakers for an event, removing the dependency on design and engineering to build a new component—reducing your cost and speed to deliver great pages. Plus, digital teams can
easily run A/B tests
, ensuring they always use the best components.
Secure Access Control
Granular roles and permissions ensure that users are only given the access they need to read, edit, or publish page types they need to perform their role, providing clear lines of responsibility and workflows.
Empowered Marketers = Happy Developers
No more dreaded “Hey, can you help me publish this?” Slack messages or unwelcome tickets. Plan sprints effectively and deliver value-add features by giving digital teams the tools they need to deliver independently.
