Scout.
The newest member of your digital team.
Scout is Uniform's AI agent. It understands your entire project and does real work from a simple request. Everything accomplishment is a draft to review before you publish. Move quickly, ensure quality, stay compliant.
Plain requests, not commands
Create a content model and the page that uses it.

Example requests, written the way you would ask a colleague.
The trust contract
Governance first
Scout will never publish. Every edit submitted as a draft, attributed and reviewable, inside the same workflows, roles, and permissions as the team it is helping.
Drafts, always.
Scout works exclusively on draft content. Publishing, workflow approvals, and everything live stays behind human decisions by design.
Attributed and permissioned.
Scout's edits are assigned to the person who asked, visible in history, and bounded by the same roles and permissions as your team.
Two ways to apply work, per task.
Review changes
Scout stages its work as pending edits you can inspect item by item before applying.
Autonomous
Scout applies the edits directly for you to review in context before publishing.
You choose per task: routine metadata can run autonomous; brand-critical work gets the review queue.

Developers
For developers: Scout is also an API
Nothing we've mentioned up to now requires writing code. This is the part that does.
ScoutClient SDK
Invoke Scout from server-side code and get structured results back, so agent work slots into your applications and pipelines.
Code automations
When the job must be deterministic, needs secrets, or calls third-party APIs, write it as code on the same triggers, and call Scout from inside it for the parts that benefit from an agent: deterministic control around an AI step.
AI-tool automations
Expose your own actions to Scout as typed, permission-scoped tools, running under the identity of whoever invoked Scout.

FAQ
Questions teams ask about Scout
Straight answers to what teams ask.
01
What is Scout?
Scout is Uniform's AI agent: a teammate inside the platform that knows your whole project (content, models, patterns, releases, optimization) and does real work from a plain request, from creating content models and pages to translating, bulk editing, and setting up tests. Every edit lands as a draft you review.
02
Can Scout publish content on its own?
No, by design. Scout works exclusively with drafts: nothing is published, no workflow advances, and nothing leaves your project until a person acts. Publishing, access, and configuration stay human decisions, which is exactly what makes it safe to let Scout do everything else.
03
What can Scout create?
Content models end to end: component definitions, content types with generated data types, and block types. And the content itself: entries, pages with their routes, pattern edits, translations, and assets with generated metadata. Ask for a landing page based on an existing one, and Scout builds it, as a draft.
04
How do we control what Scout does?
Three ways. Apply modes per task: Review changes stages every edit for item-by-item inspection, while Autonomous applies drafts directly. Permissions: Scout is bounded by the same roles as your team. And attribution: every Scout edit is assigned to the person who asked and visible in history.
05
How does Scout learn how our team works?
Through Skills (your team's reusable instructions: brand voice, checklists, conventions, loaded automatically) and AI guidance attached to specific components, content types, and fields. Write the way you work once, and Scout follows it on every request, which is how the tenth output matches the first.
06
Can Scout work without anyone prompting?
Yes, with Scout automations (early access): write instructions once, pick a trigger (a content change, a schedule, or a webhook from another system), and Scout runs them every time it fires, within the permissions you grant. Recurring content work becomes work that simply happens.
07
What does Scout cost?
Scout runs on AI credits included with Uniform plans, consumed as Scout works, whether in the dashboard or through automations. Talk to us for the allocation that fits your team's volume.
