AGENTIC AI
Meet Scout: Your AI teammate for faster, smarter digital experiences
Scout is Uniform's agentic AI that doesn't just assist—it executes. From creating new pages on command to optimizing existing experiences for SEO and conversions, Scout works alongside your team to deliver production-ready results in minutes, not weeks.
SCOUT: YOUR AGENTIC AI TEAMMATE
Scout is more than a chatbot—it's an AI agent with real skills that takes action across your entire digital experience workflow.
Please meet Scout.
SCOUT
CREATE ON COMMAND
Tell Scout what you need in plain English. "Create a product landing page with hero, features, and CTA sections" or "Generate a blog post about [topic] with SEO optimization"—Scout handles the execution from start to finish.
OPTIMIZE INSTANTLY
Scout analyzes your pages and makes them better automatically. Request SEO optimization, accessibility improvements, or performance enhancements, and Scout implements the changes without manual intervention.
PERSONALIZE & TEST INTELLIGENTLY
Scout suggests audience segments, creates personalized variations, and sets up A/B tests based on your content and goals—no manual configuration or guesswork required.
AUTO-TAG FOR SEARCHABILITY
Scout automatically tags and categorizes your content, making it instantly discoverable for your team and improving content governance.
TRANSLATE IN SECONDS
Select any composition or content entry, choose your target locale, and Scout translates everything in seconds—maintaining context, tone, and brand voice across languages.
Scout Works Everywhere
Whether you're using Uniform's full platform, adding capabilities to an existing website via EditMySite, or connecting through MCP integration, Scout adapts to your workflow.
HOW SCOUT WORKS
Scout ready to support your team 24/7.
NATURAL LANGUAGE COMMANDS
Simply tell Scout what you need in plain English. No complex syntax, no training required. "Create a product launch page," "Optimize this page for mobile performance," or "Translate this content to French and Spanish."
CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE
Scout understands your brand, your content models, your component library, and your goals. It doesn't just execute commands—it makes intelligent decisions based on your specific context and past preferences.
TAKES ACTION AUTOMATICALLY
Unlike AI assistants that only provide suggestions, Scout actually does the work. It creates components, updates content, configures personalization, implements optimizations.
SEAMLESS INTEGRATION
Whether you're using Uniform's full platform, EditMySite on an existing website, or connecting via MCP, Scout works wherever you do—no switching contexts or tools.

One AI Agent. Multiple Expert Skills. Always Ready to Help.
Think of Scout as a team member with multiple areas of expertise. Depending on what you need, Scout shifts between different skill sets:
THE CREATOR
When you need new content or pages, Scout becomes your creative partner—building components, structuring content, and assembling complete experiences based on your requirements.
THE OPTIMIZER
Need better performance? Scout analyzes your existing pages and automatically implements SEO improvements, accessibility fixes, and performance optimizations.
THE STRATEGIST
When setting up personalization or A/B tests, Scout evaluates your content and suggests intelligent audience segments, variations, and test configurations.
THE ORGANIZER
Scout keeps your content manageable by automatically tagging, categorizing, and organizing entries—making everything searchable and discoverable.
THE TRANSLATOR
Scout handles multilingual content instantly, translating compositions and entries while maintaining your brand voice across all locales.

MCP SERVER INTEGRATION
Connect Uniform with any MCP-compatible developer tool. Brainstorm in Claude, prototype in Cursor, design in your favorite IDE—then let Uniform's AI automatically structure your content, map components, and prepare everything for production.
FROM PROTOTYPE TO PRODUCTION
Start with rapid prototyping tools like v0 or Lovable, a Figma design, or even a simple text description. Uniform's AI understands your intent and transforms it into structured, production-ready digital experiences.
SEAMLESS DEVELOPER WORKFLOWS
No more copy-pasting between tools or manually recreating designs. Your creative process flows naturally into Uniform's platform, where Scout optimizes and your team collaborates.
