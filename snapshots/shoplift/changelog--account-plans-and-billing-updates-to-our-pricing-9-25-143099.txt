Updates to Our Pricing (9/25)
We've updated our pricing to reflect the growth of Shoplift over the past year.
What's Changing

Why We're Making This Change
Since early 2024, we've significantly expanded what Shoplift can do. We've introduced expanded testing capabilities with Theme, URL, and API tests, and added key workflow improvements like Test Scheduling and our Google Analytics integration. We've also continued to invest in performance, reliability, and the overall experience.
This pricing update allows us to continue building the features and improvements you've been asking for, while keeping Shoplift sustainable for the long term.
What This Means for You
Existing customers:
There are no changes to your current plan. Your active plan will be grandfathered, and you'll retain access to all the features you know and love today. If you decide to upgrade in the future, you'll move to the new pricing.
New customers:
Our updated Advanced and Pro plans will include the new features we're building in 2026, including Price Testing.
What's Next
We're actively working on exciting new features in 2026, of which Price Testing is just the beginning. Our goal remains the same: to make Shoplift the best tool for your CRO needs.
If you have questions about how these changes affect your account, reach out to us at
help@shoplift.ai
. We're happy to help.
