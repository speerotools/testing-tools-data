1. What is subscription testing?
A/B testing built for subscription brands. Every test measures subscription outcomes — attach rate — alongside overall conversion. Shoplift provides three capabilities: subscription analytics on every test, custom tests across PDP and cart, and subscription price testing via the Recharge integration.
2. Why are subscription brands underserved by general A/B testing tools?
Most tools treat shopping as a single conversion event. Subscription brands have two purchase paths — one-time and subscribe — with different economics and LTV. General platforms report in aggregate, hiding whether a test helped or hurt the subscription business. On top of that, traditional price testing doesn't work for subscription brands either — the metrics just aren't built for recurring revenue. Shoplift surfaces subscription performance separately on every test, automatically.
3. Can I A/B test subscription prices without Shoplift?
No. Until the Shoplift-Recharge integration, no A/B platform on Shopify could test actual Recharge subscription prices. Manual price changes are not tests: there's no control group and no statistical confidence.
4. What is the Shoplift-Recharge integration?
A native API integration that creates test selling plans on Recharge products with different internal discount calculations, enabling real A/B tests on subscription prices. The first time this has been possible on Shopify.
5. How does the Recharge integration work technically?
Shoplift keeps all prices for the same product in sync behind the scenes. Subscription integrity is maintained on Recharge, so you don't end up with customers on different subscriptions to manage after the test ends. Shoppers see one offer, not the test behind it.
6. How long does it take to set up subscription price testing?
The Recharge integration takes one configuration session. Once connected, you can launch your first subscription price test in minutes. The setup is one-time. Every test after that runs the same way as any other test in Shoplift.
7. How is Shoplift different from Recharge's native A/B testing?
Recharge tests how the widget looks. Shoplift tests whether the offer works, and at what price. They're complementary: Recharge covers widget presentation, Shoplift covers the full subscription experience with proper statistical rigor.
8. Do I need to migrate my existing Recharge selling plans?
No. Shoplift creates test selling plans for each test's duration. Your existing plans and current subscribers are unaffected.
9. How are existing subscribers handled during a test?
Returning subscribers stay on their existing selling plan and price. Tests apply to new purchase decisions only.
10. Does this work with subscription platforms other than Recharge?
Subscription analytics and custom tests work with any platform on Shopify Plus. Subscription price testing currently requires Recharge — the platform most serious subscription brands are already on. Support for Stay.ai, Bold, and others is on the roadmap.
11. How much does this cost?
Subscription analytics and subscription price testing via the Recharge integration are both included on Advanced and Pro plans. Core plans do not include Subscription Testing. See the main pricing page for details.
