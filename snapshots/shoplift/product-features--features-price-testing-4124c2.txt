How long does it take to see results?
Most price tests reach statistical significance within 2-4 weeks, depending on your traffic volume and the magnitude of the price change. Shoplift automatically calculates when you have enough data to make a confident decision.
Can I test multiple products at once?
Yes. You can include up to 500 products in a single test and run multiple price tests simultaneously across different catalog segments. Test your entire new collection launch or your top revenue drivers at the same time.
What happens if I update my theme while a test is running?
Nothing breaks. Shoplift integrates at the theme level, so updates, publishes, or theme switches don't interrupt active tests. Your price variants persist through theme changes and automatically apply to the new version.
Do I need developer resources to set up price testing?
Yes, but minimally. Setup requires a one-time, low-code integration that typically takes 10-15 minutes. After that, your ecomm team can launch and manage tests independently without engineering support for each experiment.
How does pricing work for price testing?
Price testing is included in our Advanced and Pro plans. Run as many experiments as you need. Contact sales to understand which plan fits your feature needs and testing volume.
