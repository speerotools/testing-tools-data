The sales assistant built for customer-facing work
Enterprise deals require dozens of custom assets to equip your buyer, more than any rep can build by hand. Mutiny creates each one from your calls, email, and CRM, so every stakeholder gets exactly what the need to move the deal forward.
1
Give every buyer exactly what they need
Create beautiful, on-brand assets that make you stand out. Be the one buyers remember with personalized decks, deal rooms, business cases, and more.
2
Equip your champion to sell for you
Arm your champions with one link they can forward to leadership: the deal room, the business case, the ROI, or the mutual action plan.
3
Start with winning playbooks
Hit the ground running with pre-loaded GTM playbooks and winning templates. Deliver value on day one instead of spending weeks on setup and tuning the agent for quality.
4
See what happens behind closed doors
Contact-level analytics show who's engaged and the moment it reaches a new stakeholder, so you know where the deal really stands.
