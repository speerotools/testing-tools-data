See what's new in GrowthBook 5.1
Go behind the scenes of GrowthBook 5.1 with the founders and engineers who built it, covering the new Slack app, hosted MCP + OAuth, contextual bandits, and more.
29 minutes
GrowthBook 5.1 is about learning faster, with new ways to scale experimentation, put AI to work across your workflow, and turn what you learn into knowledge your whole team can use.
The engineers who built GrowthBook 5.1 demo each new feature live: the official Slack app, the hosted MCP + OAuth server, contextual bandits (beta), new funnel metrics, product analytics upgrades (a rebuilt SQL Explorer, AI-generated dashboards, and user journeys), saved learnings, and composable permissions.
Visit our
5.1 release blog
for more details, or join our
GitHub Discussions
to ask questions and share feedback.
