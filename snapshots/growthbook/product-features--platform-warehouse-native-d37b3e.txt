FAQs
What is warehouse-native experimentation?
Warehouse-native means GrowthBook queries your data where it already lives (Snowflake, BigQuery, etc.) instead of copying it into our systems. Benefits: no data duplication costs, complete data ownership, use your existing metrics definitions, and total transparency into how results are calculated.
Which data warehouses does GrowthBook support?
GrowthBook works with Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift, ClickHouse, Postgres, MySQL, Athena, Presto, and more. We also support 15+ event trackers, including Segment, Mixpanel, Amplitude, and Google Analytics.
Do I need a data warehouse to use GrowthBook?
No. If you don't have a data warehouse, you can use GrowthBook’s Managed Warehouse to start experimenting immediately—no infrastructure required. Migrate to your own warehouse when you're ready.
Can I add metrics to experiments after they've started?
Yes. Forgot to track something? Add new metrics to past experiments and capture insights without re-running tests. The data is already in your warehouse. Just add the metric to your experiment and analyze the results.
How does warehouse-native help with compliance?
Your data never leaves your data warehouse. For cloud deployments, only aggregate data is shared, no PII ever leaves your data warehouse. For self-hosted deployments, you maintain complete control over all your data to comply with data residency requirements for GDPR, HIPAA, and other regulations.
