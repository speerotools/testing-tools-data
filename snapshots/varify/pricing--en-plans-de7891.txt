Frequently asked questions
Why is Varify.io® so much cheaper than other A/B testing platforms?
Varify.io® aims to make A/B testing accessible to companies of all sizes and industries. We offer a cost-effective solution that includes full functionality without limitations. Unlike other providers, we do not charge per user and instead utilize our clients' existing tracking systems, which allows us to offer our attractive pricing.
We follow the principle of the "single source of truth". We therefore do not use our own goal tracking and focus on seamless integration with existing analytics tools to ensure efficient evaluation of the A/B test results.
Who is the Growth Plan suitable for?
The Growth plan is ideal for teams that have little experience with A/B testing and are just starting out. It is perfect for websites and online stores that want to carry out A/B testing and personalization. There are no traffic limits and you have access to all platform functions. You can get started straight away without any major set-up effort.
Who is the Pro Plan suitable for?
The Pro plan is ideal for scaling teams that require advanced targeting rules, additional security features and deeper analytics. It is particularly suitable for companies that want to run complex A/B tests and personalized experiences at scale and have a greater need for analysis and optimization capabilities.
Who is the Enterprise Account for?
The Enterprise account is ideal for companies that have special requirements in terms of support as well as contractual and data protection conditions.
You will also benefit from a personal account manager who will support you in setting up new projects and answer all your questions.
Which plan should I choose if I want to test on multiple domains as a company?
Companies with several websites often have very specific requirements when it comes to A/B testing.

What does our technical support include?
Our support team consists of experienced CRO experts who will be happy to help you with the following tasks:
Set up the correct setup and targeting for your A/B tests.
Configure the connection between your web analytics tool and Varify.
Answer your questions about individual features.
What our support cannot do are special developments, extensive code analyses, advice on test design or the analysis of tracking problems of your web analysis tool that are not related to the Varify integration.
We have a network of certified partners who can help you. We will introduce you to suitable service providers and support you in your selection.
