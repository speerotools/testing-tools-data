Frequently asked questions
Will I be charged for my traffic?
–
No. With a GA4 analytics connection your tested users are unlimited. Varify bills in credits — one credit equals one domain per month, whether you test 10,000 or 10 million users.
How does Varify handle cookies and personal data?
+
Varify sets no cookies on your visitors and processes no personal data. Experiment assignment is kept in the browser of your visitor via local and session storage, and everything on our side is stored and processed in the EU. How that fits your consent setup is a decision for your own legal and privacy team; we provide the technical details and our DPA.
Do I need developers?
+
Usually not — if you can add the Varify snippet yourself, for example through your tag manager or CMS, you are set up. From there variations are built in the visual editor, with a JavaScript and CSS editor available for edge cases.
What can the AI features do?
+
Prompt Based Experiments (PBX) is the core: describe the change in your native language and AI builds the variation including its code — then you refine it in the visual editor like any other variation. On top of that, the AI CRO Audit suggests prioritised test ideas and the AI Results Summary explains an outcome in your native language.
Does Varify work with single-page apps?
+
Yes. Experiments and goals can be triggered on route changes and custom events — documented for the common frameworks.
Can I manage several domains?
+
Yes — additional domains run on additional credits, including roles and permissions per domain, which is the standard setup for agencies and group structures. Staging and UAT domains are included, so you never spend a credit on a test environment.
