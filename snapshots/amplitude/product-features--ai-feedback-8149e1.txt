Frequently Asked Questions
What is Amplitude AI Feedback?
Amplitude AI Feedback is the industry’s first user feedback engine with proprietary LLM technology that automatically turns raw customer feedback into prioritized, actionable insights.
Which integrations are supported in Amplitude AI Feedback?
Amplitude AI Feedback can connect to App Store/Google Play reviews, Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service, Gong, Trustpilot, G2, Reddit, Discord, X, and CSV/Docs, with more integrations being added all the time.
How do I get started with Amplitude AI Feedback?
Amplitude AI Feedback basic access (limited feedback volume) is available across all
Amplitude pricing plans
. Expanded record volume is available as an add-on at higher tiers.
How is Amplitude AI Feedback different from other voice of customer solutions?
Amplitude AI Feedback’s proprietary LLM technology takes a fundamentally different approach from generic language models. While most VoC solutions rely on off-the-shelf models (GPT, Claude, etc.) that were trained on general internet content, our LLM-native solution is specifically trained on product feedback patterns to automatically distill massive volumes of unstructured user feedback data into actionable lists of feature requests, complaints, and other product insights.
How does Amplitude AI Feedback address potential AI hallucination?
Amplitude AI Feedback is the only voice of customer solution that leverages proprietary hallucination detection (patent pending) to deliver accurate analysis so teams can trust the insights they receive are based on actual customer feedback, not AI imagination.
