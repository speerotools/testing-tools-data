Frequently asked questions
What do I need to get started with Amplitude Web Experimentation?
Amplitude’s
unified SDK
makes it easier than ever to get what you need to fuel your conversion rate optimization program in one platform. With a one-line script tag, you get access to Web Experimentation,
Session Replay
,
Heatmaps
, and
Web Analytics
. This gives teams a full understanding of the customer journey—what works, what doesn’t, and what’s needed to drive higher conversion.
What does Amplitude Web Experimentation come with?
With Amplitude Web Experimentation, teams can access a seamless visual experiment editor to build variants using clicks, not code. This helps teams test messaging, calls to action, and landing pages without engineering support after implementation.
How does the full Amplitude platform help fix conversion issues?
Amplitude combines no-code A/B testing,
Web Analytics
,
Session Replay
, and
Heatmaps
to show you exactly where customers get stuck in your conversion funnel. Watch users engage with your tests, identify friction points, and make targeted improvements—all without switching between multiple tools or needing help from engineering. This integrated, self-service approach helps you increase conversion rates faster and rapidly optimize the entire customer journey.
How does Amplitude Web Experimentation help with personalization?
As part of the Amplitude platform, Amplitude Web Experimentation creates powerful audience segments based on actual behavior, not just demographics or static lists. Easily design and target experiments at high-value users to deliver personalized experiences that convert. Use unified analytics to truly understand and measure the full impact of your personalization efforts.
What customer support does Amplitude offer?
We offer support plans for every stage of your growth journey. Start for free with on-demand resources like our
documentation
and the
Amplitude Academy
. Or choose from a paid plan with access to dedicated customer success and professional services teams that guide you through every step of onboarding, implementation, and
instrumentation

product metrics

How much does Amplitude Web Experimentation cost?
Amplitude Web Experimentation offers a usage-based pricing model based on the number of monthly impressions you need. Choose from
four pricing plans
. If you need more than 250,000 impressions per month, our accounts team can help you with more customized packages.
