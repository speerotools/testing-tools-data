ABlyft Lumen AI
Meet ABlyft Lumen - your AI-powered co-pilot for A/B testing. From generating experiment ideas and building variations via chat in the Visual Editor to interpreting results, Lumen helps you make smarter decisions faster and spend your time where it matters most.
Find out more
AI-Generated Test Ideas
- never run out of experiments
Stop guessing what to test next. Enter your URL, goal and target audience, and Lumen delivers up to five ready-to-run experiment ideas - complete with a hypothesis, effort, risk and confidence scores and recommended KPIs. And with every piece of feedback, the suggestions get more relevant.
AI Result Interpretation
- understand your data instantly
One click on "Interpret" and Lumen explains your results - with a clear recommendation: Ship, Keep Running or Gather More Data. Ask follow-up questions, filter by device or date range, and compare Frequentist, Bayesian and Legacy results - all in one chat.
MCP Server
- control ABlyft from any AI assistant
Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Windsurf or any other AI assistant to ABlyft via the Model Context Protocol. With 42 tools, you can create experiments, generate variations, check results and manage your entire testing program - without ever opening the dashboard.
Lumen in the Visual Editor
- just describe your variation
You know what you want to change, but not how? Just tell Lumen. With "Edit with AI" on any element or "Build with AI" in the toolbar, you describe your change in your own words - and Lumen applies it live on your page right away.

All AI Features at a Glance
ABlyft Lumen brings artificial intelligence directly into your experimentation workflow - so you can focus on growing your business, not on manual tasks.
Experiment Ideas Generation
Enter your URL, select your goal, your target audience and whether you want content or feature tests - and receive up to five AI-generated ideas with a hypothesis, effort, risk and confidence scores and recommended KPIs. Everything lands in your idea backlog, and Lumen learns from every rejection.
AI Result Interpretation
A conversational AI agent that analyzes your A/B test results and gives you clear recommendations: Ship, Keep Running or Gather More Data. Start with suggested questions, filter by date or device type and compare Frequentist, Bayesian and Legacy.
AI in the Visual Editor
With "Edit with AI" and "Build with AI", you simply describe changes in words - Lumen applies them live in the preview. Refine step by step, combine AI and manual changes and remove any AI change individually. Credits are only charged when you save.
Variation Code Generation
Describe the variation you want in your AI assistant and Lumen generates the matching CSS and JavaScript code and creates the variation directly in ABlyft. Reliable selectors and proper waiting for elements included.
MCP Server Integration
Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Windsurf or any other MCP-compatible AI assistant to ABlyft via OAuth. With 42 tools, you control experiments, variations, pages, audiences, goals, schedules, exclusion groups and results through natural language.
AI Credits & Usage Tracking
Keep full control of your AI usage with a transparent credit system. See exactly how many credits you've used, what's left this month and who used which feature when - with warning levels at 70% and 90% and filters by type, source and user.
AI Opt-In & Privacy Controls
Stay in control of your data. AI is off by default - only the team owner can enable it or disable it permanently. No personal data is processed, no data is used for AI training, and an amendment to the data processing agreement is available on request.
Security Guardrails
Lumen only sees what it really needs: form values, password fields, email addresses, phone numbers and tokens are removed or masked before sending. Generated code cannot send data anywhere or access cookies or browser storage.
ABlyft Lumen - Your AI Identity
All AI responses come from ABlyft Lumen, your dedicated A/B testing AI assistant. Lumen focuses exclusively on experimentation expertise and responds in your language - so you always get relevant, context-aware answers.
What are AI Credits and how do they work?
Every AI action uses credits from your monthly allowance: experiment ideas cost 100 credits per run (up to five ideas), result interpretation 50 credits per chat message, code generation via MCP 50 credits, and saving new AI changes in the Visual Editor 50 credits per save. Your credits reset automatically every month. You can track your usage anytime in Team Settings - including a warning at 70% and 90%.
Is my data safe when using ABlyft Lumen?
Absolutely. AI features are opt-in: only the team owner can enable, disable or permanently turn them off. Only the minimum necessary information is sent to the AI provider - sensitive content such as form values, email addresses or phone numbers is removed or masked beforehand. Your data is never used for AI training.
What is the MCP Server and how do I connect it?
The MCP Server (Model Context Protocol) allows AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Windsurf to work with ABlyft directly. Simply add the server URL from your personal settings as a connector and authorize the connection via OAuth. You then get 42 tools for experiments, variations, pages, audiences, goals, schedules, exclusion groups and results - always with exactly the permissions of your user.
Do I need technical knowledge to use the AI features?
Not at all. ABlyft Lumen is designed to be intuitive for everyone. Whether you're generating test ideas, building variations via chat in the Visual Editor, interpreting results or using the MCP Server - Lumen guides you through the process and responds in your language.
Do I pay for every chat message in the Visual Editor?
No. In the Visual Editor, you can chat with Lumen and refine your changes as much as you like. Credits are only charged when you save a variation with new or modified AI changes - a flat 50 credits per save, no matter how many changes it contains.
Will Lumen overwrite my manual changes?
No. AI changes and manual changes are managed separately and can be combined in the same variation. Lumen only ever adjusts its own changes - and you can remove any AI change individually without losing the rest of your variation.
