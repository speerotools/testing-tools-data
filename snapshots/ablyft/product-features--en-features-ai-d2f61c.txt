ABlyft Lumen AI
Meet ABlyft Lumen - your AI-powered co-pilot for A/B testing. From generating experiment ideas to interpreting results, Lumen helps you make smarter decisions faster and spend your time where it matters most.
Find out more
AI-Generated Test Ideas
- never run out of experiments
Stop guessing what to test next. Just enter your URL and goal, and Lumen delivers five ready-to-run experiment ideas - complete with hypotheses, effort scores and recommended KPIs. More experiments, less brainstorming.
AI Result Interpretation
- understand your data instantly
Get clear, actionable recommendations for every experiment: Ship, Don't Ship, or Keep Running. Ask follow-up questions, filter by device or date range, and compare Frequentist vs. Bayesian results - all in a conversational interface.
MCP Server
- control ABlyft from any AI assistant
Connect Claude, ChatGPT or any AI assistant to ABlyft via the Model Context Protocol. Create experiments, generate variations, check results and manage your entire testing program - without ever opening the dashboard.
All AI Features at a Glance
ABlyft Lumen brings artificial intelligence directly into your experimentation workflow - so you can focus on growing your business, not on manual tasks.
Experiment Ideas Generation
Enter your URL, select your goal and target audience - and receive five AI-generated experiment ideas with hypotheses, effort and confidence scores and recommended KPIs. Ready to implement in minutes, not days.
AI Result Interpretation
A conversational AI agent that analyzes your A/B test results and gives you clear recommendations: Ship, Don't Ship, or Keep Running. Ask follow-up questions, filter by date or device type and compare statistical methods.
Variation Code Generation
Describe the change you want to make and Lumen generates the CSS and JavaScript code for your experiment variation. It identifies the right DOM elements, creates reliable selectors and explains every change it makes.
MCP Server Integration
Connect your favorite AI assistant to ABlyft via the Model Context Protocol. With 17 tools available, you can create experiments, manage variations, retrieve results and control your entire testing program through natural language.
AI Credits & Usage Tracking
Keep full control of your AI usage with a transparent credit system. See exactly how many credits you've used, what's left this month and who used which feature when - all in a clear dashboard under Team Settings.
AI Opt-In & Privacy Controls
Stay in control of your data. Team admins can enable or disable AI features at any time. No personal data is processed, no data is used for AI training - and a clear data processing agreement is in place.
ABlyft Lumen - Your AI Identity
All AI responses come from ABlyft Lumen, your dedicated A/B testing AI assistant. Lumen focuses exclusively on experimentation expertise and responds in your language - so you always get relevant, context-aware answers.
What are AI Credits and how do they work?
Every AI action uses credits from your monthly budget. Generating experiment ideas costs 100 credits, while result interpretations and code generation each cost 50 credits per request. Your credits reset automatically every month, and you can track your usage anytime in Team Settings.
Is my data safe when using ABlyft Lumen?
Absolutely. AI features are opt-in and can be enabled or disabled by your team admin at any time. No personal data is processed, and your data is never used for AI training.
What is the MCP Server and how do I connect it?
The MCP Server (Model Context Protocol) allows external AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT to interact with ABlyft directly. With 17 available tools, you can manage experiments, create variations and retrieve results - all through your preferred AI assistant.
Do I need technical knowledge to use the AI features?
Not at all. ABlyft Lumen is designed to be intuitive for everyone. Whether you're generating test ideas, interpreting results or using the MCP Server - Lumen guides you through the process and responds in your language.
