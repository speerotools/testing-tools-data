Frequently Asked Questions
Will page speed be impacted by the installation of the tracking code?
No, the installation of Compose's tracking code won't impact your page speed. We leverage AWS Lambda@Edge to ensure high-performance testing without causing any flicker. Detailed instructions for optimal performance are available in our support center.
How easy is it to implement? What onboarding resources are available?
Implementing Compose is straightforward and user-friendly. We provide various onboarding resources, including a complimentary 1-hour session, email support, and access to our extensive support center with tutorial videos. For additional assistance, we have an Advanced Support package and offer half or full-day support sessions with our technical team.
Is any development work required to launch tests?
This depends on your website’s setup and the types of tests you want to run. We offer a no-code Shopify integration for easy A/B testing of themes and templates. Additionally, our split URL variant type allows you to create variants using external page-builder applications enabling no-code experiments. For teams with developers, we provide a code editor and options for custom goals to facilitate more complex experiments.
Does Compose allow server-side testing?
Not at this time.
How much does Compose’s experimentation software cost?
Compose is the only A/B testing software with an entirely pay-as-you-go pricing model. It costs $0.0012 per monthly tested user (MTU). Read more about our pricing information.
