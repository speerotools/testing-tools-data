The Most Affordable A/B Testing Software for Maximizing ROI
Let's face it, A/B testing software can be a jungle. Many companies shroud their pricing in mystery, requiring multiple meetings before revealing a quote that lands in the thousands per month. Even worse, you’re often forced to sign 1-2 year contracts locking you into a hefty investment before even testing out the tool yourself. In this article, we compare Compose’s pricing to some of the more affordable and pricing-transparent A/B testing options on the market. Keep in mind, the prices in this article are subject to change. For the most current and accurate pricing information, you will need to visit the competitor websites.
At Compose, we believe transparency is key. That's why we offer the most affordable and effective experimentation, with a simple, pay-as-you-go pricing model: $0.0012 per tested user, always. You only pay for what you use each month, allowing more flexibility and ultimately saving you money. If you accumulate 114K monthly tested users (MTUs), we charge you for 114K instead of tiered models that force you to upgrade your plan and pay for 250K or 500K.
Pricing Comparison
But you’re not only saving by paying for fewer MTUs, Compose’s rate per MTU is also lower than the competition's. Here's how Compose stacks up against Convert and VWO at exactly 100K tested users.
Compose: $120/Month | $0.0012/Tested User
Convert: $499/Month | $0.00499/Tested User, over 4x per user
VWO: $604/Month | $0.00604/Tested User, over 5x per user
