Frequently Asked Questions
Can I use Compose if I’m not on Shopify?
Yes, Compose is platform agnostic and can be utilized on any website. However, for teams operating on Shopify, we offer a dedicated integration to facilitate A/B testing of themes and templates within your Shopify account.
How do I integrate Compose with my Shopify store?
Check out our support center for comprehensive instructions on our Shopify integration, as well as resources and tutorial videos demonstrating how to use Compose with Shopify.
Do I need development knowledge to use Compose?
No development knowledge is required to utilize Compose with our Shopify integration. You can effortlessly create no-code experiments by splitting traffic between themes and templates using Shopify's editor. However, advanced features are available for teams with development expertise, enabling customization through custom goals, Javascript variants, and more.
What if we only want to run experiments occasionally?
No problem! Compose operates on a pay-as-you-go model, allowing you to adjust your experimentation velocity as needed without any penalties. You're only billed for the monthly tested users you accumulate, so if you decide to pause experiments for a month, you won't incur any charges.
