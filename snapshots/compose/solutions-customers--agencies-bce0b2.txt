Why agencies love Compose
Compose was built with agencies at the forefront. We’ve got you covered with a pricing model designed to save you and your clients money, tools to encourage collaboration, and easy client management.
Agency overview video
Pay as you go pricing model
The one and only pay per MTU solution. Save you and your clients money by only paying for the monthly tested users that you actually need. Perfect for websites with fluctuating traffic and varying experimentation velocity.
Easy client management & collaboration
Manage and review all of your client’s experimentation roadmap, tests, and results in a single dashboard. Plus add unlimited collaborators with complex role designation options to ensure you can work with your team efficiently.
Limitless and scalable experimentation
No limitations on experiments, projects, or collaborators. Since Compose is a pay as you go A/B testing tool you never need to worry about surcharges or plan upgrades so you can keep your focus on driving growth through experimentation.
CRO strategic planning tools
Provide extra value to your clients with a hypothesis list and roadmap to present the upcoming CRO plan. Plus make more of your experimentation by keeping testing strategically focused.
