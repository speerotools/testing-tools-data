Monthly Release Notes - April 2026
Referrals Now Available in Convert
We released the
Referrals
feature in Convert to support the Convert Ambassadors initiative.
This new functionality helps incentivize collaborators to recommend Convert, creating more opportunities to expand awareness and support growth through referrals.
New “Exists” and “Does Not Exist” Conditions in Audience Rules
We added
Exists
and
Does Not Exist
conditions to the
Audience Rules
for visitor cookies.
This improves targeting flexibility by giving teams more control when building audiences based on whether a cookie is present or missing.
OAuth Clients Released for Secure App Connections
We released
OAuth Clients
in the app, allowing users to register and manage external applications that connect securely to Convert using OAuth authentication.
This makes it easier to support secure integrations and gives users more control over how third-party applications access the system.
Microsoft Clarity Integration Now Much Easier to Use
While the
Microsoft Clarity
integration was not newly released in April, it was significantly improved.
We removed much of the manual setup process and made the integration far more automatic, making it easier than ever to connect Convert with Microsoft Clarity.
Cloudflare SDK for Edge Experimentation
We introduced the
Cloudflare SDK
, enabling teams to run A/B tests and personalization directly on
Cloudflare Workers at the edge
.
The SDK handles visitor identification, experiment assignment, caching, and response modification closer to the user. This enables faster server-side experimentation with minimal latency and no flicker, while reducing reliance on slower client-side execution or origin-server logic.
Improved Tracking Script Performance
We improved the efficiency of our tracking script so it now loads faster and has less impact on website performance.
While this update may not be directly visible in day-to-day usage, it reflects our continued focus on optimization and performance. It is also part of our broader push around
Monthly Performance Improvements
in the in-app dashboard.
