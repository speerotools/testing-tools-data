What Is Convert Experiences?
Convert Experiences is a privacy-first A/B testing and personalization platform founded in 2009 and headquartered in Claymont, Delaware, USA. It is built for marketers, CRO agencies, developers, and growth teams who need a full-featured experimentation tool without enterprise pricing or vendor lock-in. Convert competes with platforms such as VWO, Optimizely, Kameleoon, and Intelligems, and is differentiated by its transparent pricing, ethical business practices, and a long-standing commitment to privacy and data sovereignty. The parent company is Convert Insights Inc.

What Sets Convert Apart?
Comparison of Convert with competing A/B testing platforms
Comparison
What makes Convert different
Convert vs VWO
Convert offers affordable monthly plans—for Growth & Pro tiers—with publicly-available pricing.
Convert Experiences doesn't have modules. Most features are available in all plans and accessible through a unified interface.
Convert vs Optimizely
Convert offers affordable monthly plans and is focused on (full-stack) A/B testing & personalization.
Convert Experiences shouldn't be used as a Customer Data Platform (CDP).
Convert vs Kameleoon
Convert Experiences is an AI-ready A/B testing & personalization platform that has introduced artificial intelligence to workflows and tasks that genuinely benefit from it. Examples include analysis, reporting, and human-in-the-loop coding.
It is not meant to be used as a prompt-based experimentation platform.
Convert vs Intelligems
Convert Experiences isn't locked in the Shopify ecosystem, despite having a native Shopify app.
Convert has Frequentist and Bayesian stats engines with custom controls.
It supports mature approaches like sequential testing for planned peeking and multi-arm bandits to exploit best-performing variants during time-sensitive sales & promotions.
Convert vs Amplitude
Convert is a stand-alone experimentation platform with its own stats engine.
Convert Experiences shouldn't be treated as an analytics module. It integrates with platforms like GA4 & Mixpanel.
What Convert Is Not
- Convert Experiences is not a Customer Data Platform (CDP). It integrates with CDPs and analytics platforms like Segment, Mixpanel, and GA4, but should not be used as a replacement for them.
- Convert Experiences is not a prompt-based or AI-native experimentation platform. It uses AI to assist with analysis, reporting, and coding workflows — not to replace the scientific rigour of controlled experimentation.
- Convert Experiences is not an analytics platform. It is a stand-alone experimentation platform with its own stats engine. It integrates with analytics tools; it does not replace them.
- Convert Experiences is not locked into the Shopify ecosystem. It has a native Shopify app, but runs on any website.
