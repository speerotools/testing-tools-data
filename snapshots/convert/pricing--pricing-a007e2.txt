Are Convert Experiences plans inclusive of taxes?
+
−
All plan prices shown exclude VAT/GST. Applicable taxes will be calculated at checkout, based on your billing location, when you choose and purchase a plan in the app.
What are tested users?
+
−
Tested users are the number of site visitors participating in A/B tests you have running on the Convert Experiences platform.
Let’s break down how we count users.
The people who come to your site are termed as your
site visitors
. Not all site visitors though are Convert app
tested users
.
Users
are specifically the people who are bucketed into tests that you have running on your site. In counting users, we do one key thing.
We disregard overlaps
. So if there are 100,000 site visitors who see Test #1, and another 100,000 visitors who see Test #2… the total app tested users will be:
200,000 people if the two sample sizes (of 100,000 each) have no overlaps. That means
no one person
has seen both tests.
Less than 200,000 if the two sample sizes (of 100,000 each) have overlaps. If both sample sizes have 50,000 people who are unique to one test and 50,000 people who have seen both tests, then the tested users count will be 150,000.
Tested users count is thus the same as (or less than) your website’s total unique site visitors, depending on how you choose to bucket your visitors into tests.
This method of counting unique users replaces the older (pre April 2020) counting of unique tested visitors which took overlaps into consideration and gave a net tested visitors count based on all the visitors to each test.
What does technical support cover?
+
−
While we want to help you with each and every action item on our platform, the truth is – this is neither feasible, nor a good idea.
Expert technical assistance is dispensed by (human) support magician who’ve been using our app for years.
As such support includes:
Pointing you in the direction of the right features to use
Helping you use these features well
Troubleshooting the process of feature usage (as needed)
What our technical assistance can’t cover are instances when
you need custom development to make a feature work, exhaustive code analysis and review, advice on test design.
We have an amazing network of Convert certified agency partners and experts who step in and expedite the process. We can introduce you to these partners, and help you choose the service provider who will be the best fit.
Convert has never offered in-house testing & development services (out of respect for our agency customers), and we never will.
Can I cancel my subscription easily?
+
−
Yes. We have a very simple and transparent monthly plan option.
You can cancel at any time during that month to stop the billing process without any stipulations.
Your data will remain available in your account for three months, just in case you decide to reactivate our services. When you select a yearly plan you can cancel at any moment and your plan will not be renewed when the yearly contract is over.
Will you cancel my account if we get too many tested users in a day/month?
+
−
No we won’t.
A/B testing should support growth, and we do not penalise you for growing.
In case of a spike in the number of Monthly Tested Users to your experiments, we will keep your account open and signal when the count of tested users reaches 90% and 100% respectively. After this you can always purchase additional monthly tested users (MTUs) at $399 per 100k users for the Growth plan, and $699 per 250k users for the Pro plan.
What are Deploys in the Convert Experiences ecosystem?
+
−
A Deploy (personalization) is a series of changes on a page or pages geared towards a specific audience, without an Original/Variation structure and without a Convert reporting dashboard.
Deploys can also be used by marketing and growth teams for the following:
1:1 personalizations (effective in ABM plays) using CSV-provided visitor or group data and placeholders/variables to personalize text, images, colors, HTML, CSS, or JS.
No-code changes to content management systems (CMS) when developer resources aren’t readily available.
Quick roll-outs of A/B test winners as personalizations, until the changes can be hard-coded. This isn’t recommended as a way to bypass properly implementing test winners.
What are active projects, domains and goals?
+
−
Within the Convert Experiences app, the word Active is used to indicate any project, domain or goal that is currently in use. For example, if you opt for our Kickstarter plan, you can add several main domains to your account. But, you can only test on 5 of them at a time.
Do you track multiple domains?
+
−
Yes. We offer cross-domain functions for goal tracking, test and target, and we offer all this out of the box. We will support any number of websites, domains, intranets, extranets, and e-commerce sites.
What AI capabilities does Convert Experiences offer?
+
−
Convert Experiences supports AI-assisted experimentation through an AI Wizard for variation copy recommendations, code-editor quality checks for precise experiment code, and the latest Convert MCP Server for securely connecting AI tools like Cursor or Claude to Convert data, documentation, reporting, and — when permissioned — experiment creation and management.
My site receives 80 million pageviews. Can your platform handle it?
+
−
