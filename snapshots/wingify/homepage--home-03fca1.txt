Still deciding?
Here is what teams usually ask before they start
What products are included in the Wingify platform?
The Wingify platform includes six product offerings that run on one shared foundation:
Experimentation: Website experimentation built for non-technical teams
Personalization: Real-time adaptive personalization
Analyze: Heatmaps, session recordings, funnels, and surveys
Feature Management: Feature flagging, rollouts, and server-side experimentation
Commerce: Semantic search, product & content recommendations, and merchandising
Engage: Behavior-triggered browser push notifications
How is Wingify different from buying separate specialized tools?
Separate tools each hold a slice of the true customer picture, so insight sits in one place and action in another. Wingify runs analytics, experimentation, personalization, feature management, and engagement on one customer profile, so that you learn about users and fix their friction in the same place.
What is Wingz AI?
Wingz AI is Wingify's agentic AI layer that carries the context & intelligence across all Wingify products.
It runs your optimization loop as one continuous cycle — understand, decide, act, and learn — so every visitor gets the most relevant experience for them, and you move from insight to action faster, autonomously.
You set the objectives, strategy, and guardrails, and Wingz helps you act faster than a team could alone.
It operates across experimentation, personalization, user behavior analysis, and feature rollouts.
Do I need developers to use Wingify?
You don't need developers for most of Wingify's web experimentation and personalization work. After a one-time install of the SmartCode snippet, marketers and product teams build tests, target audiences, and launch personalization within the platform. Server-side experiments and feature management, however, use Wingify's SDKs, which developers integrate once in your codebase and ship feature builds, after which product teams or engineers can manage feature configs from the Wingify dashboard. So we cater to each team based on how they prefer to work.
How long does it take to get started with Wingify?
Getting started with Wingify's website optimization products is just one step:
Install the SmartCode snippet on your site.
For Feature Management, you need to integrate an SDK in your codebase based on your tech stack. Website behavior analysis, experimentation, and personalization can go live the same day, with no engineering team required after SmartCode installation. Feature Management, however, you would need to make some code-level configurations, that may take longer.
You can start on a 30-day free trial with no credit card, or book a demo for a guided setup. Onboarding support and product training come included.
Is Wingify GDPR compliant, and how is my data secured?
Yes. Wingify is committed to complying with the GDPR and other applicable data protection laws. We have established comprehensive privacy and security programs designed to protect customer data and support GDPR requirements, including data subject rights, contractual data protection commitments, international data transfer safeguards, and appropriate technical and organisational measures. Wingify has a dedicated global Data Protection Officer (DPO) who oversees our privacy and data protection program and supports compliance with applicable data protection requirements worldwide.
Security and privacy are built into our products, processes, and infrastructure by design. We use measures such as encryption for both data at rest and in-transit, pseudonymisation, anonymisation, access controls, and ongoing security assessments to protect the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and privacy of customer data. Our security and privacy practices are also independently assessed against recognised standards by third-party reputed auditors, including ISO 27001 ISMS, ISO 27701 PIMS, ISO 27017 Cloud security, ISO 27018 Cloud Privacy, PCI DSS v4.0.1 and SOC 2 Type II.
We are committed to transparency and continuously strengthen our security and privacy practices as regulations, technologies, and threats evolve.
