Frequently Asked Questions
Here is what teams usually ask before they start
What is Wingify Experimentation?
Wingify Experimentation helps teams create, target, measure, analyze, and roll out web experiments, including A/B tests, multivariate tests, split URL tests, and AI-assisted experiments.
What types of tests can I run?
Wingify Experimentation lets teams run several types of experiments, including:
- A/B tests
- Multivariate tests
- Split URL tests
- Multi-page experiments
- Cross-domain experiments
- Device-specific experiments
- AI-assisted tests with Wingz
Do I need developers to launch experiments?
No, it’s not necessary to have a developer. Teams can use no-code tools to update copy, layouts, CTAs, banners, styling, and page elements visually. Developers can support more advanced use cases when needed.
How does Wingify Experimentation help ensure reliable results?
Wingify Experimentation supports reliable decision-making with goals, guardrails, health checks, mutually exclusive groups, and reporting, helping teams reduce overlap, monitor risks, and trust their results.
Can I target experiments to specific audiences?
Yes, Wingify Experimentation lets teams target visitors using:
- Traffic source
- Device
- Behavior
- Custom events
- Third-party data
- Lifecycle stage
- Reusable audience conditions
Can I understand why a variation won or lost?
Yes, heatmaps and session recordings add behavioral context, helping teams understand what happened and why.
