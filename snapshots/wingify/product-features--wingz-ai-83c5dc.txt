Your AI team for the entire optimization loop
Wingz brings agents, workflows, and connected AI into one place, so your team can move from insight to action faster. Wingz runs the whole optimization loop as one continuous cycle — understand, decide, act, and learn — so every visitor gets the most relevant experience, in the moment.
Understand
See every visitor, not just the ones who logged in.
Wingz reads the signals across your customer journey for every visitor, including the anonymous ones. Intent, Affinity, Engagement, History and more. One clear picture of each visitor powers everything: what Personalization serves, what Commerce ranks, what Engagement times.
Decide
The right thing, for the right person, right now.
Wingz chooses what to show to whom, against the objective you set — which variant, which ranking, which rollout step, which channel and moment.
Act
From hypothesis to something a visitor sees.
Variations built. Audience picked. Shelf ranked. Flag staged. Message sent. And before any of it goes live, it's checked on synthetic visitors, shipped inside your guardrails, with a kill switch armed.
Measure & Learn
Every result makes the next one smarter.
Every action runs against a permanent holdout, then gets analyzed — which segments behaved differently, what moved in the funnel, what the replays show. The learning is stored, and it feeds the next proposal.

Frequently Asked Questions
Here is what teams usually ask before they start
What makes Wingz different from other AI tools?
Wingz is built specifically for digital optimization and experimentation. It is woven into the Wingify platform, where it can access your analytics, heatmaps, session recordings, campaigns, and workflows to act on real product and customer data. General-purpose assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are built for broad, open-ended conversation and knowledge tasks. Wingz is built to help you find opportunities, run better experiments, and turn results into your next strategic move.
Do I need a developer or data scientist to use Wingz?
No. Wingz AI is designed for optimization, product, and marketing teams to use directly. You can prompt it in plain language, click any element on a page to make a change, upload reference files, and get data-backed ideas and reports without writing code or running analyses.
Where a developer should step in: for advanced integrations, custom data sources, complex targeting logic, or connecting Wingz to your own systems via APIs or MCP, a developer can extend what Wingz can reach and do.
Will Wingz make changes to my site automatically?
No. Wingz handles analysis and suggestions. Your team decides what gets tested and when. Nothing goes live without your explicit approval.
Can Wingz work with my existing analytics tools?
Yes. Connect GA4, Mixpanel, Amplitude, and Notion directly inside Wingz for unified analysis, without leaving the platform.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Wingz follows strict privacy and security standards and does not use your customer data to train global models. Sensitive data is masked automatically. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 compliant.
Is Wingz included in my plan, or does it cost extra?
Wingz Core is automatically enabled for all paid Wingify accounts. Wingz Advanced, Credit Packs, and the Enterprise Data Control add-on are activated on request through your Wingify Account Manager.
Which AI models power Wingz, and can I bring my own LLM?
With Wingify’s MCP, you can run Wingz from any AI agent you trust. This includes a one click integration with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Gemini. With our Enterprise Data control, you have the option to Bring Your Own Model (BYOM).
What's the difference between a Wingz Agent and a Wingz Workflow?
With Wingz, an Agent is a specialized AI you configure once to handle a repeatable optimization task — like analyzing a campaign, segmenting across regions, or generating test ideas — and it runs that work the way you would, every time.
A Workflow is a broader, customizable automation that chains triggers, actions, and one or more Agents together.
In short: an Agent does a specific job, while a Workflow orchestrates multiple steps and Agents to automate an entire process.
