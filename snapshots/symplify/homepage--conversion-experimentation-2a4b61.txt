A/B Testing
Off the shelf A/B testing functionality that will power any number of comparative A/B tests on your website. Read more about A/B testing and how best to develop a coherent test strategy here. A/B testing is synonymous with modern web development. In a simple A/B test, two different versions of a page, an “A” version and a “B” version, are sent to two similar sets of random viewers. This is also known as A/B/n or Split testing. The A and B versions may be totally different, or may differ only in key areas; for example, the headline, image or call to action. The goal of an A/B test is to determine which version performs best. Metrics that may be evaluated include email opt-ins, link clicks, bounce rate, or purchases.

Heatmaps
Heatmaps take large amounts of visitor data and turn it into easily understandable visual snapshots of your website. These snapshots show you the website content with which visitors interact. This includes content which remains unseen because visitors do not scroll far enough, as well as unused elements which can be removed or replaced. Heatmaps are useful for quickly identifying patterns and trends in data, as well as for identifying areas of the data that may be of particular interest or concern. They can also be used to communicate data to others, such as through the use of heatmap visualisations in reports or presentations.
Read more
Visitor Recording
Visitor recordings, also known as session recordings or user recordings, allow you to record and replay actions as users navigate through your website. By capturing mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling, you’ll gain deeper insight into actual visitor behaviour.

FAQs
How does Symplify’s AI personalization work?
Symplify uses machine learning and continuous data streaming to deliver predictive, one-to-one personalization messages. The platform analyzes live behavioral data to generate AI-driven recommendations, detect churn risk and trigger automated journeys instantly. Personalization adapts across channels and on-site as customer behavior changes.
Does Symplify support A/B and multivariate testing?
Yes. Symplify includes built-in testing tools that allow marketers to compare variations of messages, journeys and segments to find the highest-performing version. Also offering control group selections.
