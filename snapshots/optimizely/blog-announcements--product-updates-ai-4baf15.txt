Q4 roadmap
Our product team moves fast, continuously shipping new features that are driven by what real marketing and digital teams need to work smarter. This page keeps you current on what's new and what's next.
Virtual Teammates Across Your Stack
Work with Virtual Teammates in the tools your team uses every day. Assign work and track progress across Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, and more, while every request, decision, and output stays visible and auditable.
Mobile & Desktop Apps
Keep work moving from wherever you are. Native apps for desktop and mobile let you review, approve, and run agents on the go, and alert you the moment a Virtual Teammate needs your input.
Voice Mode
Speak naturally and interact with Mark entirely hands-free. Mark transcribes your ideas in real time, reads its responses aloud, and automatically stays listening so you can keep the conversation flowing without ever touching your keyboard.
New Agents
Continuous additions of pre-built agents that are easy to leverage and customize for your specific needs.
Agent Directory
Human and Agent-in-the-loop
Collaborate confidently with agents, and virtual teammates, by embedding native approval checkpoints directly into your workflows. Review, edit, or reject agent outputs with full context and inline controls, ensuring nothing ships without your final sign-off.
Mark Models
Models tuned on the work marketers actually do, so quality goes up as cost comes down. Early tests show 10x better value than raw frontier models, with every output grounded in your brand, data, and history.
Goal Trees
Define what success looks like so AI works toward the outcomes that matter. Map how company and team goals connect, link them to measurable results, and give agents the structure to prioritize what moves the business.
Tools and MCPs
Power your agents with real-time data from across your entire marketing stack. Seamlessly connect Optimizely to essential tools to build highly informed, action-oriented workflows.
See all connectors
Artifact Collaboration
Comment on any brief, doc, image, or email and have Mark make the edits for you, so feedback stays in context instead of scattered across email and chat. Humans and Virtual Teammates work from the same version, cutting feedback loops between first draft and final sign-off.
Tracker & Activity Stream
A shared board and activity panel show your to-dos next to your Virtual Teammate's work. You can see what it's doing now and what's coming next at a glance.
Pre-built Workflow Agents
Pre-built, multi-step workflow agents that power AI features across Experimentation, CMS, and CMP that update automatically when we ship improvements.
Create Workflow Agents from Chat
Describe what you need in plain language and Mark builds the multi-step workflow agent for you. To change an existing agent, just ask. No developers needed.
Live Agent Progress in Chat
Initiate long-running agent jobs and follow their progress live in a side panel in Chat. Meanwhile, you can move on to other topics in the same thread.
Artifact Tab Improvements
Search, filter, pin, and delete files, images, and presentations. Past work is quicker to find and your workspace stays clean and easy to share from.
Organization Credit Limits
Admins have the option to set a hard credit limit for your whole organization in Admin Center. The platform will pause automatically once purchased credits run out, so AI spend never goes over budget.
Workflow Webhook Triggers
Start agent workflows in real time from events in Teams, Zoom, Outlook, and Google Calendar. The event's details go to the agent so it starts with the full picture, with no custom code.
Plugins
Install a complete use case in one go, with every agent, skill, and connector it needs bundled from the directory (e.g., Translation Agent + Term Bank Skill).
Agent SDK
Call Agent Platform agents and workflows from your own apps and scripts and get the finished answer back, without rebuilding the logic. Agents can be managed as code through your CI/CD process.
