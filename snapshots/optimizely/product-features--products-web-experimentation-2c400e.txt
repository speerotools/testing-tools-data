Questions you might have
What is Agentic Experimentation?
keyboard_arrow_down
Agentic Experimentation is a smarter way to run experimentation program
s
with AI. It helps teams move faster across the full experimentation lifecycle, from generating test ideas and
prioritizing
opportunities to building variations, checking quality,
analyzing
results, and deciding what to do next.
What types of experimentation does Agentic Experimentation support?
keyboard_arrow_down
Agentic Experimentation supports both web experimentation and feature experimentation. Web experimentation helps teams test and
optimize
digital experiences on the front end, while feature experimentation helps product and engineering teams test server-side changes, manage feature flags, and roll out new capabilities safely.
Can I integrate Agentic Experimentation with other tools?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Optimizely is designed to work with the tools teams already use across marketing, product, engineering, analytics, and content workflows. This helps teams connect experimentation with the broader digital experience stack rather than working in silos.
How does Agentic Experimentation help teams run better experiments?
keyboard_arrow_down
Agentic Experimentation helps teams improve both speed and quality. It can surface stronger test ideas, turn insights into structured plans, help create variations faster, support QA, and recommend next steps based on results, so teams can spend less time on manual work and more time driving impact.
Is Agentic Experimentation built for marketers or developers?
keyboard_arrow_down
Both. Marketers can use Agentic Experimentation to generate ideas, build stronger test plans, understand results, and move faster from insight to action. Developers can use it to support more technical experimentation workflows, including feature experimentation, server-side tests, flags, and controlled rollouts.
How does Agentic Experimentation help increase conversion and revenue?
keyboard_arrow_down
Agentic Experimentation helps teams identify, launch, and learn from the tests most likely to improve customer experiences. By focusing experimentation on higher-impact opportunities, teams can optimize the journeys that influence conversion, engagement, and revenue.
Can Agentic Experimentation help with personalization?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Agentic Experimentation can help teams understand which experiences work best for different audiences, then use those learnings to create more relevant customer journeys. It helps teams move from broad testing to smarter, evidence-based optimization.
Can Agentic Experimentation recommend what to test next?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Agentic Experimentation can help teams identify high-potential opportunities by using behavioral signals, past experiment results, and performance data. Instead of starting every cycle with guesswork, teams can prioritize ideas based on evidence.
