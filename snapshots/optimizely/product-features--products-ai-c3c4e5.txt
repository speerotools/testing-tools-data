Questions you might have
What is Optimizely Agent Platform?
keyboard_arrow_down
It's an agent orchestration platform built for marketers. Create, govern, and scale specialized AI agents without writing a single line of code. Leverage your agents across Optimizely's products or within your own tech stack.
How does it integrate with the tools I already use?
keyboard_arrow_down
Optimizely's agents are woven directly into your existing workflows. It operates through a unified layer across all Optimizely products and sits as an AI layer across your existing martech stack.
What kind of tasks can Optimizely's agents help me with?
keyboard_arrow_down
Optimizely Agent Platform comes with a directory of 50+ pre-built, specialized agents ready to work 24/7. Examples include:
GA4 Report Generation:
Automatically surfaces insights on traffic, behavior, and conversions.
Blog Post Generation:
Accelerates the creation of high-quality blog content, from headlines to conclusions.
Content Adaptation:
Reuses content across different channels and audiences while maintaining brand voice.
Experiment Planning:
Speeds up the process of building and documenting experimentation plans.
GEO Recommendations:
Audits your webpages to optimize them for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
Or, create your own for any use case you dream up.
Is my data safe when I use Optimizely Agent Platform?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Optimizely provides robust AI governance and security. Your data (prompts, inputs, and outputs) is processed in-memory and is
not persistently stored
in the LLMs we use.
Crucially, your data is
never used to train
any general models, and you
retain full ownership
of all AI-generated content.
How do agents help improve marketing performance?
keyboard_arrow_down
Optimizely Agent Platform is designed to deliver measurable results. By automating tasks, we've seen customers free up
3-5 hours weekly per marketer
. It helps you create personalized content that can
improve engagement by over 50%
and
cuts production time in half
. It also provides instant compliance checks,
saving hundreds of review hours.
How do agents help scale content creation?
keyboard_arrow_down
Agents move you beyond simple text generation, and into scalable, on-brand content creation. Take it a step further and create 1:1 personalized experiences for every buyer, all with the help of Optimizley's AI agents.
How are Optimizely's AI agents different from other AI assistants?
keyboard_arrow_down
While most AI tools are just chat interfaces, Optimizely has a complete
agent orchestration platform
. The key difference is your ability to build, customize, and chain multiple specialized agents into complex workflows that run autonomously. It’s not just an assistant; it’s a system for building and managing your own AI workforce.
Do I need to be a technical user to use Optimizely Agent Platform?
keyboard_arrow_down
No, it's built for marketers! You can deploy and orchestrate powerful AI agents without the need for developers. The process is straightforward:
1.
Create your agent
(or use a pre-built one).
2.
Give your agent tools
(like access to your analytics or CMS).
3.
Build your workflow
using a drag-and-drop editor.
How can my team get started?
keyboard_arrow_down
To get started, you can
request a demo
to see it in action or contact your account manager to learn how to activate it for your team.
What is the long-term vision for Optimizely Agent Platform?
keyboard_arrow_down
The vision is to empower marketers to move from manual execution to automated orchestration. By enabling you to build an autonomous AI workforce that operates 24/7, it helps handle your repetitive tasks while allowing your team to focus on strategy and creativity. The result is autonomous workflows and endless outcomes.
