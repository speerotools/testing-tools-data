Frequently asked questions
Do you offer a free trial or plan?
We have a generous free plan that lets you get started with website personalization for up to 10,000 MAU. Visit our
plan comparison
section to see the features included in this free tier and explore our other options for higher user volumes.
What is monthly active users (MAU)?
This is the number of unique users who interact with your website in a month, regardless of whether a personalized experience or active experiment impacts them. Each plan has an MAU quota with the flexibility to purchase additional volume as needed.
Do web crawlers and bots count toward my MAU limit?
By default, known bots and web crawlers do not count toward your MAU limit on paid plans, since we provide a
bot filtering feature
that excludes this traffic from your experiences and experiments. However, if you choose to disable the bot filtering feature, we'll treat bots as regular users and will include them in your MAU count.
What happens if my website hits the MAU limit?
In this case, you can upgrade your plan to accommodate more users, enable the Pay As You Grow (PAYG) feature in your current plan to cover the extra users, or do nothing. If you choose not to take action, the service will be unavailable to your application (meaning it won't track events, deliver personalization, default content, or AB testing variants) until you adjust your plan or the plan automatically renews for the following month. You will still be able to access the admin panel during this time.
What is a slot, and how does it work as a content placeholder?
A
slot
is a context-specific placeholder in your application that defines where content goes and what type of content can be displayed. While
components
are the reusable building blocks (like a hero section), slots are the specific placeholders (like a home hero) where those components are rendered.
Are there limits on the number of experiences or experiments I can run?
Quotas vary by plan. Visit our
plan comparison
section to see the quotas for each plan.
What if I need more features or want to increase my quotas?
You can upgrade your plan to a higher tier that provides the features and user volume you need. Alternatively, if you have specific needs beyond our standard plans, our sales team is happy to help! Feel free to
contact us
to discuss add-ons or explore custom plans that may better fit your situation.
What are the benefits of choosing an annual plan?
Annual plans often come with special pricing and financial benefits compared to monthly subscriptions.
What payment methods are available?
Currently, we only accept credit card payments for our subscriptions. However, if you have specific payment needs or require a different method, please
contact us
and we will explore alternative options that may work for you.
Can I switch my plan at any time?
You can cancel your subscription at any time unless you have a special agreement or commitment, such as a custom plan or negotiated contract, which may have specific cancellation terms. Please refer to your contract for cancellation policy details or
contact us
for more information.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time for all standard plans. Custom plans may have different terms and conditions. Also note that switching from an annual plan to a monthly plan may have limitations, as annual plans often include special offers tied to the commitment.
Is there a partner program if we manage Croct for multiple clients?
Yes, we have a
partner program
designed for agencies that manage multiple clients. It is specifically structured to help you shift from one-off projects to generating recurring revenue by offering conversion rate optimization services.
