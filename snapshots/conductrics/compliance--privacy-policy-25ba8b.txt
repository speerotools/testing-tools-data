What we collect and why
What:
Contact form info (name, email, company, message)
Why

We do not collect personal identifiers for analytics, track you across other sites, sell your data, or use advertising cookies. Find further details about these activities below.

handling your inquiry.

Nobody else. We do not sell or share your data.

Our use of analytics
What we do
We use
privacy-first analytics
to understand how our website performs and to improve the user experience. Our analytics collect only anonymous, aggregated data.
What we collect
Pages visited and visit duration
Referral source (how you arrived at our site)
Device type and browser
Country-level geographic location (city-level maximum)
Page load time and performance metrics
Bounce rate, scroll depth, and click statistics
What we do NOT collect
Personal identifiers (name, email, IP address retained beyond geolocation)
Cross-site browsing history
User profiles or behavioral fingerprints
Technical safeguards
Cookie lifetime
: Maximum 13 months (non-rolling)
Data retention
: Maximum 25 months
IP anonymization
: IP address anonymized immediately after city-level geolocation
Cross-site tracking
: Disabled — no tracking across other websites or applications
Third-party data sharing
: None — analytics data is never shared with third parties
Data cross-referencing:
Analytics data is not combined with any other data sources
Opt-out
Although consent is not required, you can opt out of analytics at any time. Use the opt-out mechanism available on our website. Once you opt out, no analytics data will be collected from your visits.
Our use of A/B testing
What we do
We use
privacy-first A/B testing
to optimize our website content and design. A/B testing measures the relative performance of different versions of a page to help us improve the experience for all visitors.
What we collect
Which variation of a page you were shown
Aggregated interaction metrics (clicks, conversions) at the cohort level
What we do NOT collect
Personal identifiers
Individual-level behavioral profiles
Data that enables targeting of unique users
Technical safeguards
Cohort-level only
: No individual targeting; every analysis is performed at variant × cohort level
K-anonymity threshold
: Results are computed and retained only once the cohort for a given variant reaches
k ≥ 5 distinct visitors
. Below that threshold, no segment-level data is produced.
No re-identification
: A/B cohort assignment is not cross-referenced with any other data source
Cookie lifetime
: Maximum 13 months (non-rolling)
Data retention
: Raw cookie-level assignment data is held only for the duration of the test; only aggregate k-anonymised results are retained (maximum 25 months)
Third parties
: No sharing outside our analytics processor (Piwik PRO), which is bound by a written data processing agreement
