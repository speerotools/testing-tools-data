Experimentation
Experiment across the full experience
Pure client-side approaches are limited to cosmetic changes, while server-side releases often create bottlenecks. Conductrics handles both fluently. You can run visual
A/B and multivariate tests
, alongside complex backend experiments, managed from a single interface.
Adaptive Optimization
Optimize with transparent machine learning
Automating decisions often means sacrificing transparency. We believe you should never have to guess why a model acted. Our contextual bandits
maximize real-time value
, discovering segments for
adaptive personalization
. With human-readable decision trees, you ensure performance without losing strategic control.
Active Research
Diagnose user intent within your platform
Behavioral data tells you what happened, but leaves you guessing about the why. Conductrics solves this by triggering
targeted surveys
directly inside your experiments. When a user abandons a task, you can ask them why immediately, linking direct customer response data to your analysis.
