AI capabilities in SitecoreAI
In addition to the features available in
Agentic studio
, SitecoreAI provides AI-powered assistants that help marketers create, improve, personalize, and translate content.
Powered by generative AI, assistants are built directly into SitecoreAI to support everyday marketing tasks. They can suggest ideas, generate content, refine existing content, and complete actions in real time.
You can use assistants to:
Improve content in real-time
Personalize pages
Support A/B testing and optimization
Create
deliverables
and
tasks
Create smart briefs
using the AI Assistant to generate content and timeline suggestions
Provide brand guidance through the
Brand Assistant
Translate an
entire site
or a
page
with AI.

You can
assign a brand kit to a site
and use it when generating, optimizing, or translating content.
Assistants and Agentic studio agents
Assistants and Agentic studio agents support different types of marketing work:
Capability
Best for
How it helps
Assistants
Quick, interactive tasks such as drafting, editing, personalizing, or summarizing
Speeds up everyday work and helps keep content consistent
Agents
Multi-step processes that require automation and coordination
Coordinates tasks and workflows across tools
In summary:
Use assistants for quick creative support.
Use agents to automate complex marketing processes.
Best practices for working with SitecoreAI
To get the most from the AI capabilities built into SitecoreAI, follow these best practices:
Start with a goal
- know what you want to achieve before using AI.
Be specific
- when required for inputs, give clear prompts and include important context.
Stay involved
- treat AI as a helper, not a replacement.
Check results
- always review outputs for tone, accuracy, brand, and intent.
Refine as needed
- don’t settle for the first result; iterate.
Use data
- apply real-time insights to keep content relevant.
Keep oversight
- humans should always approve key decisions.
Start small
- begin with simple AI tasks, then grow over time.
Track progress
- review how AI is performing and adjust if needed.
Stay ethical
- protect sensitive data and respect content ownership.
