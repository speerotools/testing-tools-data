The questions we hear most
What is conversion optimization?
Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the process of improving a website or app to increase conversions, meaning the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action such as completing a checkout, signing up, clicking a call-to-action, or making a purchase. Conversion optimization works by analyzing user behavior through tools like heatmaps, testing changes to design, copy, layout, and messaging, and measuring which version lifts the conversion rate. SitecoreAI supports conversion optimization by connecting real-time personalization, on-site search, A/B/n testing, and page-level analytics on the same live visitor data.
What is a conversion rate optimization (CRO) strategy?
A conversion rate optimization strategy is a structured plan for testing hypotheses to increase conversions from existing traffic and improving a website conversion rate. It works by analyzing user behavior, identifying barriers in the conversion funnel, testing improvements, and measuring the lift in conversion rate. A CRO strategy is more focused than a general optimization strategy: it targets the average conversion rate, such as purchases or sign-ups, while a general optimization strategy targets broader website performance metrics like number of visitors, load times, bounce rates, and overall usability across the user journey.
What is landing page optimization?
Landing page optimization is the process of improving a landing page to increase the percentage of visitors who take a specific action, such as signing up or making a purchase. It refines elements like design, messaging, layout, and calls to action to lift the landing page conversion rate. Landing page optimization is usually part of a broader digital marketing strategy, helping improve the conversion funnel, prevent drop-off, solve pain points in the first-time customer journey, and improve the customer experience.
What is a good conversion rate?
A good conversion rate is the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action, and it varies by industry benchmarks, audience, traffic source, and customer journey. A higher conversion rate and increased number of conversions usually signal more effective marketing, a clearer conversion funnel, stronger user experience, and a better-optimized landing page.
What should buyers look for in a conversion optimization platform?
Buyers should look for a conversion optimization platform that connects live data, personalization, experimentation, on-site search, and analytics in one place. The platform needs real-time audience signals, controlled A/B/n experiments, page-level insight, and the ability to roll winning experiences out across connected sites and campaigns. SitecoreAI brings these capabilities together, with Audience and Insights providing the live visitor context and functionality that Conversion Optimization uses to act on intent and lift the conversion rate.

SitecoreAI Conversion Optimization connects real-time personalization, on-site search, A/B/n experimentation, and page-level analytics in one platform, running on the same live visitor data. Teams act on live visitor intent in real time and test what works, then roll winning experiences out across every connected site in a single click. Because it runs on the same data as Audience and Insights, every personalization rule and every test reacts to visitor behavior the moment it happens, with no data syncs between separate tools.
What signals does SitecoreAI Conversion Optimization use, and how does it test and roll out?
SitecoreAI Conversion Optimization uses live behavioral signals to read visitor intent and respond in the moment. Those signals include on-site searches, clicks, scroll depth, and other on-site interactions, all captured natively on the platform without external tracking or third-party tags. For testing, you run A/B/n and multivariate experiments on any page variant or content block inside Page builder, then deploy winning variants across every connected site in a single click. Learnings compound over time, with the same live visitor data feeding every test, so each experiment informs the next.
