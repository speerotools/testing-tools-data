Get Started
Welcome to the ABsmartly Documentation.
ABsmartly is an advanced experimentation and feature flagging platform designed to help teams validate ideas quickly, measure impact reliably and roll out changes safely. The platform provides everything you need to design experiments, expose users consistently, measure impact with robust metrics and make evidence based product decisions.
This documentation will guide you through how to use the platform, integrate it into your product and understand the core concepts behind trustworthy experimentation.
