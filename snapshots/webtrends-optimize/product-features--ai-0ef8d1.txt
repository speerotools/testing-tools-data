What do you mean by AI with Purpose?
We see glorified ChatGPT Wrappers embedded into many software platforms, and don't want to have "AI for the sake of it". Nor something that sounds good on paper but doesn't actually work. So when we use AI, it's to help you go faster, think broader or do more, in very specific areas. Not just a chatbot sat on top of some help docs.
