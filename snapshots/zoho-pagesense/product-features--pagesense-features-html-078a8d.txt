Security and Privacy
Roles
Assign the right roles to your team members to ensure they have the necessary permissions to manage experiments and reports.
HIPPA compliance
PageSense secures sensitive data and ensures compliance with HIPAA regulations when handling personal health information (PII).
GDPR
Zoho PageSense complies with GDPR standards, protecting user privacy and securing personal data.
Audit log
Record a detailed log of all actions performed within Zoho PageSense in setting experiments to enhance security, track changes, and support compliance requirements
Privacy protection settings
Ensure visitor privacy by masking sensitive information such as names, card details, passwords, email addresses, and home addresses in PageSense session recordings. By default, all keystrokes and inputs are replaced with asterisks (*). Also, selectively mask specific elements and areas to further protect user data while analyzing session recordings in PageSense.
