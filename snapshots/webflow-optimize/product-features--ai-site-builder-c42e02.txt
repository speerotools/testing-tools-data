Frequently asked questions
Can I use my own domain when building my site with AI?
Yes! You can use your own domain to house any Webflow site, whether you build it yourself, with the AI site builder, or with one of our hundreds of templates.
To connect your domain, you’ll need to have a paid Site plan. If you don’t yet own a custom domain, you can buy one directly within Webflow or from any domain name provider.
How do I build a website with AI?
One of the easiest ways to use AI to build a website is with a tool like Webflow’s AI site builder, which can transform your idea into a beautifully designed, fully functional website in minutes. Once your site is ready, you can continue using Webflow AI to edit, optimize, and scale it as your business grows.
What else can I do with Webflow AI?
Beyond using it to generate a site, you can use Webflow AI to help you generate new pages and CMS Collection items, optimize with native SEO and AEO features, and even answer your questions as you build — all without leaving the Webflow editor.
What is an AI website builder?
AI website builders like Webflow’s generate a professional website from start to finish — including a functional layout, images, and copy — based on a prompt that you give it. Using an AI site builder speeds up the process of developing a new website, making it a great option for businesses and individuals that want to launch quickly.
What should I look for in an AI site builder?
A strong AI site builder should do more than generate a single web page. It should create a complete, multi-page foundation for your website, including a reusable design system to help your site look and feel consistent as it grows.
Which framework are AI site builder sites built on?
Sites created with Webflow’s AI site builder are built on
Webflow Flowkit
, Webflow’s modular CSS framework. Flowkit provides a structured system of reusable utilities, components, and variables to ensure that your site’s design is customizable, consistent, and scalable well beyond the initial AI-generated build.
