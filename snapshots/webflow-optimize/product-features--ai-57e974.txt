FAQ
How does Webflow use AI?
Webflow weaves AI throughout our entire platform to provide users with powerful tools to help build, manage, and optimize web experiences.
Generative AI capabilities show up in Webflow in a few ways:
In-context actions ​​appear directly in the flow of work to speed up design, content creation and management, and optimization.
The Webflow AI Assistant, an intelligent conversational experience that partners with users to reason through tasks, take action, and answer questions without leaving the platform.
Webflow also offers tools powered by machine learning technology:
Webflow Optimize uses machine learning to automate experimentation and personalization.
Webflow Localization uses machine learning to translate and adapt sites for global audiences.
These AI capabilities help teams move from idea to impact faster.
What can Webflow AI do?
Webflow helps teams build, manage, and optimize web experiences by embedding AI directly into their workflows to accelerate site creation, generate and refine copy, and create structured CMS content at scale, all while staying on brand.
Webflow AI also supports ongoing site improvement: extending experiences with new sections and code; improving reach and relevance through SEO and AEO; improving performance and messaging over time; and localizing experiences for global audiences.
Along the way, Webflow AI provides guidance and recommendations in context — reducing friction, increasing confidence, and helping teams move faster from intent to impact.
Does Webflow have an AI website builder?
Yes! Webflow’s AI site builder helps users generate an entire website with a scalable design system in minutes, all from a basic prompt — and without the need for design or technical expertise. This is a great option for businesses that want to launch sites quickly while maintaining creative control. Learn more about the AI site builder here.
Can I integrate third-party AI tools with Webflow?
Yes. Webflow’s AI capabilities can be extended through the Webflow Marketplace and the Webflow MCP server to grow your AI toolkit without leaving the platform. You can integrate AI-powered apps like Adobe Express for visual content creation, Jasper for on-brand content generation, and Relume for site building. The Webflow MCP server enables secure connections between Webflow and your IDE or agent platform of choice to streamline CMS management, SEO, site maintenance, and more.
What makes Webflow's approach to AI different from other website builders?
Unlike other website builders, Webflow AI helps users build sites faster and more easily,
without
locking them into rigid templates or preventing additional customization through code. And Webflow AI extends beyond the initial site build, with capabilities like A/B testing, personalization, smarter content management, and more that help teams scale and improve performance over time.
Are there any limitations to Webflow's AI features?
Webflow AI helps with every phase of building, managing, and optimizing web experiences. Access to Webflow AI features varies by workspace, site plan, and the purchase of the Webflow Optimize and Webflow Localization add-ons. Some capabilities are subject to usage limits
How do I get started using Webflow's AI features?
Access to Webflow’s AI features varies by workspace, site plan, and the purchase of the Webflow Optimize and Webflow Localization add-ons. For those with access, generative AI features are enabled by default, but can be opted out of at the workspace level. To get started using AI features, just click the AI icon that appears in navigational elements and menus throughout Webflow.
How do AI credits work?
As AI becomes more central to how teams manage their most important marketing properties, we've introduced a credit-based system to ensure customers have built-in access to the tools they need.
AI credits are available on all Workspace plans. Your allotment of AI credits is based on your Workspace plan type. For our self-serve plans, AI credits will reset monthly. For the Team & Enterprise plans, AI credits will reset yearly.
Only a subset of Webflow AI features consume AI credits. As we extend this credit-based system to other existing capabilities, or launch new ones, we will be transparent about which capabilities will require the use of credits.
You can track usage of AI credits in the AI usage dashboard. If you need additional AI credits, you can purchase AI credit add-ons at any time.For more information, visit our
