Is Omniconvert Commander the same as Nexus by Omniconvert or Reveal?
+
Yes. Reveal became Nexus by Omniconvert in 2026, and Nexus by Omniconvert became Omniconvert Commander in October 2026. It is the same product, with the same team, data and features. Only the name changed.
What is eCommerce AI automation?
+
eCommerce AI automation uses AI to replace the manual marketing decision layer: it detects growth opportunities, generates ad creatives and campaign packages, and optimizes spend for real profitability. Omniconvert Commander connects to your eCommerce stack to identify what to test and to measure True Profit instead of ROAS. Launch-ready asset generation is in beta.
What is True Profit vs ROAS?
+
ROAS measures revenue per dollar of ad spend but ignores cost of goods, shipping, returns, discounts and platform fees. True Profit subtracts all these costs to show actual bottom-line contribution. A campaign with 3x ROAS can be deeply unprofitable once real costs are deducted. Omniconvert Commander calculates True Profit for every campaign, ad, and product.
Does Omniconvert Commander replace my existing tools?
+
No. Omniconvert Commander is an orchestration layer, not a replacement. Keep Triple Whale, Klaviyo, Meta Ads and Gorgias. It reads these tools through read-only connections today and adds the intelligence layer on top: it turns raw signals into prioritized actions and measures what moves the bottom line. In beta, approved work goes back to Meta, landing pages and on-site overlays. Your tools stay. It adds the brain.
Who is Omniconvert Commander built for?
+
Omniconvert Commander is built for Shopify brands doing $5M–$200M in annual revenue that have already assembled a marketing stack but feel like they’re spending more time operating tools than growing the business. If your growth team spends more time coordinating tools than growing the business, it was built for you.
Does Omniconvert Commander work with WooCommerce or BigCommerce?
+
No. Omniconvert Commander is built for Shopify stores only.
What makes Omniconvert Commander different from a regular analytics dashboard?
+
Dashboards show you what happened. They hand you a chart and leave the interpretation to you. Omniconvert Commander tells you what to do about it: every insight surfaces with a ranked profit impact, a ready-to-approve hypothesis, and a pre-filled campaign brief. And it does not stop at the recommendation: in beta, it builds the campaign, ads, landing page and experiment to act on it.
How does Omniconvert Commander reduce eCommerce CAC?
+
Three ways: it identifies which campaigns are truly profitable after COGS, shipping, and returns. In beta, it monitors competitor ad libraries to find untapped messaging opportunities. Also in beta, it keeps one message across ads and landing pages, which removes the mismatch that inflates acquisition cost.
How does Omniconvert Commander handle ad creative fatigue?
+
Creative fatigue tracking is in beta for selected customers. Omniconvert Commander tracks creative decay curves: monitoring CTR decline, CPC increases, and frequency saturation. It forecasts when each creative will fatigue and tells you how many new creatives you need next month, before CAC climbs. Proactive replenishment instead of reactive scrambling.
How quickly will I see meaningful results?
+
The first value is visibility: within days of connecting your data, Omniconvert Commander surfaces anomalies and profit leaks that most brands don’t spot for months. Actionable experiment recommendations follow within the first week as it learns your account patterns. Campaign packages and approval-based launch are in beta. Broader autonomous deployment is coming next.
Can Omniconvert Commander help with brands that have seasonal products?
+
Yes. Today, Omniconvert Commander reads demand signals from GA4 and Google Ads. The Seasonality Engine is coming next: it will analyze historical Shopify orders, GA4 traffic patterns, and Google Ads data to forecast demand peaks 6–8 weeks ahead. Rather than reacting to a Black Friday spike, you will brief creatives and build budgets while competitors are still planning.
Does Omniconvert Commander improve ROAS, or will it actively reduce it?
+
Possibly both, and that’s intentional. If you’re currently scaling campaigns that look good on ROAS but are unprofitable after COGS and returns, Omniconvert Commander will surface that and recommend reallocation. Reported ROAS might drop while actual profit increases.
What is customer lifetime value optimization?
+
CLV optimization means making decisions based on total profit a customer generates over their entire relationship, not just the first purchase. Omniconvert Commander segments your customers by RFM and connects every campaign to its CLV impact.
Can Omniconvert Commander help me identify which customer segments to prioritize?
+
Yes, and this is one of Omniconvert Commander’s core differentiators. It segments your customer base by RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary value) and identifies Champions, Loyals, At-Risk, and Dormant customers. Every insight, hypothesis, and campaign recommendation is linked back to its segment-level CLV impact.
What is RFM segmentation and how does Omniconvert Commander use it?
+
RFM stands for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value: the three strongest predictors of future purchase behavior. Omniconvert Commander automatically places every customer into a segment: Champions, Loyals, Potential Loyals, At-Risk, and Dormant. Every campaign it recommends is tied to how it will move customers between these segments.
How does Omniconvert Commander use NPS and review data?
+
NPS scores and review sentiment are fed into the CX Intelligence module, where they’re parsed by AI agents and ranked by profit impact. A recurring complaint about a specific product category becomes a ranked insight with an estimated revenue impact. Your customers are essentially writing your next experiment for you.
Can Omniconvert Commander help reduce customer churn?
+
Yes. Omniconvert Commander monitors RFM segment movements in real time. When a cohort of previously loyal customers starts slipping toward At-Risk, an anomaly alert fires with the estimated CLV at stake. It can then propose a win-back campaign sequence. Pre-generated email copy and ad variants are in beta.
Does Omniconvert Commander work with Shopify?
+
Yes. Omniconvert Commander is built for Shopify and Shopify Plus stores. It integrates with Shopify for product and order data, Meta Ads and Google Ads for campaign performance, GA4 for visitor analytics, and Klaviyo for email and SMS. All connections use read-only access.
Do I need a developer or technical resource to get started?
+
No. Connecting your data is handled through standard OAuth flows: the same kind of “Connect with Shopify” button you’ve used dozens of times. No code, no API keys, no developer required. The entire setup takes under 1 hour.
What if I don’t use Klaviyo, Triple Whale, or some of the listed integrations?
+
Omniconvert Commander works with what you have. The minimum viable connection is Shopify + Meta Ads or Google Ads. Every integration you add increases the quality of insights, but you don’t need all of them to get value.
Can I connect multiple Shopify stores?
+
Multi-store support is on the roadmap and will be available for brands operating multiple Shopify properties. Right now, Omniconvert Commander supports a single store. Founding brands with multiple stores are invited to share their setup so we can prioritize the architecture.
How does Omniconvert Commander handle data from unsupported platforms?
+
For platforms not yet natively supported, the team evaluates integration requests from founding brands on a case-by-case basis. Demand from founding brands is the primary input into the integration roadmap. In the interim, GA4 acts as a universal proxy for many behavioral signals.
Is my data safe? What does read-only access mean?
+
Read-only access means Omniconvert Commander can see your data but cannot make changes to your accounts, pause campaigns, or move budget without your explicit approval. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can revoke access at any time from within each platform.
Does Omniconvert Commander store my customer data on its own servers?
+
Omniconvert Commander processes aggregated metrics and anonymized behavioral signals, not raw PII. Customer names, emails, and payment details stay entirely within your existing platforms. We are compliant with GDPR and CCPA by design.
Is Omniconvert Commander GDPR and CCPA compliant?
+
Yes. Compliance is built into the architecture. Omniconvert Commander operates on aggregated, anonymized data signals and never processes raw personal information beyond what is necessary. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available on request.
Can I revoke Omniconvert Commander’s access at any time?
+
Yes, immediately and unilaterally. Each platform connection is granted independently through that platform’s own permission settings. Revoking access immediately stops any further data ingestion.
Will my data be used to train AI models for other brands?
+
No. Your data is used exclusively to generate insights and recommendations for your own account. Omniconvert Commander does not use one brand’s data to train or improve models deployed for another brand. Each brand’s intelligence layer is isolated.
What can Omniconvert Commander do today?
+
Data connections, anomaly detection and opportunity ranking are live. Creative intelligence, campaign packages, landing page generation and approval-based action flows are in beta for selected customers. Broader autonomous campaign deployment is coming next.
How much does Omniconvert Commander cost?
+
Omniconvert Commander installs free from the Shopify App Store. The Founding Free plan includes 900 credits a month. Paid plans are Creator at $49, Growth at $199 and Scale at $499 a month, billed through Shopify. Unlimited is on a custom quote. The pricing page lists what each plan includes.
What if I connect today and decide Omniconvert Commander is not right for me?
+
No penalty, no obligation. Connecting your data starts with read-only access. If you decide Omniconvert Commander isn’t right for you, you revoke access and walk away. There is no contract.
Is there a minimum contract length?
+
No minimum term. Omniconvert Commander is designed to be kept because it generates measurable profit impact, not because you’re locked in.
