At AI speed, control in production is essential.
Without Runtime Control
Ship and hope: no control once changes hit production.
Issues are discovered only after users are impacted.
Rollbacks are slow, manual, and disruptive.
Every incident depends on someone jumping in to fix it.
With Runtime Control
Ship with control: pre-defined guardrails, success metrics, and automated rollouts.
Change-level issues are detected immediately—before users are impacted.
Remediation is instant, automated, and non-disruptive.
Incidents are handled—no one needs to jump in.
Quickstart Guide
CodeControl
01
Define the change and guardrails.
Create a feature and tie it to key system and business metrics. Define thresholds for performance, errors, or user impact before release.
02
Release and observe in production.
Roll out changes gradually while the system continuously monitors performance and acts automatically at the first sign of issues.
03
Contain and correct, automatically.
Pause, revert, or adjust instantly when thresholds are crossed—without redeploying or manual intervention.
