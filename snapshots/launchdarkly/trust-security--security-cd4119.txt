Data privacy and our EU residency.
01
Data privacy.
Protecting the privacy of our customers and their users is of the utmost importance to us. LaunchDarkly does not require any personally identifiable information (PII) to be passed to our service. We operate our data privacy program in accordance with privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA.
LaunchDarkly provides comprehensive privacy and security protections that enable our customers to use our products in compliance with HIPAA and
FedRAMP

02
EU Region Available.
As European companies face increasing scrutiny over data management, having an EU hosted solution that supports EU data residency becomes essential. This infrastructure not only supports compliance but also empowers organizations in regulated industries like finance and energy to innovate with confidence, knowing their data is secure and accessible.
