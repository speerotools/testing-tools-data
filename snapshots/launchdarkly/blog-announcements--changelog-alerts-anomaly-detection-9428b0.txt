Alerts: Anomaly detection, custom messages, and inline graphs
Alerts in LaunchDarkly Observability now support
anomaly-based triggering
,
custom messages with Markdown and templating
, and
inline graphs in notifications
. You can also use a new
Test alert
button to preview notifications before they fire.
Previously, alerts relied on static thresholds. With anomaly detection, alerts can trigger on meaningful deviations in your metrics without manually tuning thresholds. Custom messages let you include structured context and runbook links, while inline graphs (for example, in Slack) give responders immediate visibility into metric trends.
Availability
GA for customers using LaunchDarkly Observability.
