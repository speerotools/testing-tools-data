Developer
This topic explains LaunchDarkly plans you can subscribe to and summarizes the features available on each. LaunchDarkly offers four tiers to choose from to fit your organization’s size and needs.
Read the pricing page for up-to-date plan information
To learn the latest details about LaunchDarkly plans and pricing, read
Pricing
.
​
Plan features
LaunchDarkly offers Developer, Foundation, and Enterprise plans, as well as a Guardian add-on for Enterprise plans:
Developer
plans help you get started with feature flags for passion projects. Developer plans are free to use and don’t require a credit card.
Foundation
plans include scalable feature management and Experimentation for growing teams.
Enterprise
plans support advanced software delivery practices at Enterprise scale.
The
Guardian
add-on provides additional features for Enterprise plans to monitor and guard your releases at scale.
You can subscribe as a developer for free with limited usage of
service connections
,
MAU
,
Experimentation keys
, and observability features (
session replays
,
errors
,
logs
, and
traces
). If you subscribe to a Foundation or Enterprise plan, monthly entitlements are included for each of these.
You can add
Data Export events
to Foundation and Enterprise plans as an add-on.
You can upgrade your organization’s subscription at any time. For more information about pricing and feature support, read
Pricing
. For assistance with right-sizing your account,
start a Support ticket
or read
How to right size when you are over LaunchDarkly system resource count limits
.
​
Guarded rollout availability
All LaunchDarkly accounts include a limited number of guarded rollouts to help you evaluate the feature in real-world scenarios. You can use these guarded rollouts on any environment and for any feature flag.
After you use the included number of guarded rollouts, you must upgrade to an Enterprise plan with the Guardian add-on to create new ones.
To learn more, read
Guarded rollouts
.
​
Free trials
When you sign up for a free trial with LaunchDarkly, your trial includes access to all of the LaunchDarkly Enterprise and Guardian features.
When your trial ends, your account will automatically convert to a free Developer plan. At that time, LaunchDarkly retains any account data related to Guardian features, but you will not be able to interact with those features unless you upgrade to an Enterprise plan with the Guardian add-on. You can also choose to upgrade to a paid Foundation or Enterprise plan.
Here is how your account features change with each plan type:
If you upgrade to an Enterprise plan with the Guardian add-on, you will retain access to all Guardian features and any Guardian configuration you completed as part of your trial, including setting up
Guarded rollouts
.
If you upgrade to an Enterprise plan without the Guardian add-on, you will retain access to all Enterprise features and any Enterprise configuration you completed as part of your trial, such as setting up
teams
or
roles
. LaunchDarkly retains any account data related to Guardian features, but you cannot interact with it.
Any guarded rollout already configured on a flag will be available to start, and any guarded rollouts still in progress will complete. You will not be able to create new guarded rollouts on new flags.
If you move to a Developer or Foundation plan, LaunchDarkly retains any account data related to Enterprise and Guardian features, but you cannot interact with it.
Any guarded rollout already configured on a flag will be available to start, and any guarded rollouts still in progress will complete. You will not be able to create new guarded rollouts on new flags.
Any LaunchDarkly members that you assigned a custom or preset role will revert to the base role they had before you assigned the custom or preset role, such as the Reader base role.
The
Relay Proxy in offline mode
will still function, but you will not be able to download new flag rules.
You can also
delete your trial account
, which permanently removes your account data.
