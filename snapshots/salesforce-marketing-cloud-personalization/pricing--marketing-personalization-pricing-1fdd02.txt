Personalization FAQ
What is Salesforce Personalization?
Salesforce Personalization is our next-generation solution, built natively on the Headless 360 platform. It enables businesses to deliver 1:1 real-time personalized experiences across every customer touchpoint—driving stronger relationships, higher productivity, and increased revenue.
Powered by connected data, advanced AI models, and Agentforce, Salesforce Personalization pulls data from any source, infers customer intent, and makes instant, trusted decisions aligned with both customer and business goals.
Built on Salesforce’s Einstein Trust Layer, it ensures secure data handling and gives marketers full visibility and control over AI-driven personalization. Use cases include activating real-time product and content offers on the web, dynamically tailoring email content, and enabling Agentforce to adapt browsing experiences and respond to customer questions in real time.
What is Marketing Cloud Personalization+?
Marketing Cloud Personalization+ is a pricing edition that includes both Marketing Cloud Personalization and Salesforce Personalization.
Marketing Cloud Personalization is our personalization solution built on Marketing Cloud (formerly Interaction Studio). Salesforce Personalization is our next-generation solution, built natively on the Headless 360 platform and powered by Agentforce and Data 360.
Marketing Cloud Personalization+ is available to both new and existing customers who want to use both solutions for their personalization use cases. For existing customers, this edition enables you to explore and activate the latest innovations without disrupting your current Marketing Cloud Personalization setup. You can deploy the Marketing Cloud Personalization and Data 360 web SDKs side by side to run Salesforce Personalization use cases on the same site—and even reuse your existing Marketing Cloud Personalization sitemap to define event collection for the Data 360 Web SDK module.
I’m already using Marketing Cloud Personalization. How do I access the new Salesforce Personalization capabilities?
You can access Salesforce Personalization by renewing or swapping to Marketing Cloud Personalization+.
I’m a new customer. Can I buy Marketing Cloud Personalization+?
Yes. MCP+ is available to both new and existing customers. New customers can start with the bundled edition to gain flexibility in how they implement personalization strategies—whether through Marketing Cloud Personalization, Salesforce Personalization, or both.
What’s included in Marketing Cloud Personalization+?
Marketing Cloud Personalization+ includes:
Full access to all Marketing Cloud Personalization functionality
Full access to all Salesforce Personalization functionality, including Agentforce decisioning, AI objective-based recommenders, custom attribution, and cross-channel personalization
50 million personalization credits (for Salesforce Personalization decisioning)
Do I need Data Cloud to use Salesforce Personalization or MCP+?
Yes. Data 360 must be provisioned to access both Salesforce Personalization and Marketing Cloud Personalization+.
Salesforce Personalization is built on the Salesforce Platform and relies on the real-time customer profile from Data 360 to power AI-driven personalization. Since Marketing Cloud Personalization+ includes Salesforce Personalization, Data 360 provisioning is required for all customers—whether new or existing.
Can I use Marketing Cloud Personalization and Salesforce personalization data across my other Salesforce application (in addition to Marketing Cloud)?
Yes. Salesforce Personalization's intelligent AI decisioning engine powers product and content recommendations, experimentation, and attribution services that can be used across channels (web, email, mobile) and integrated with other Salesforce products, including Agentforce, Service Cloud, Sales Cloud, and Loyalty Cloud.
For example:
Add personalized recommendations to your service console and Agentforce to help human and digital workforces quickly resolve customer inquiries and turn support conversations into upsell opportunities.
Help sellers engage customers more effectively by surfacing personalized recommendations directly within the Contact record.
Use personalized recommendations to select and deliver the ideal content for each loyalty promotion.
