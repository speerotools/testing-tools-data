What does it do?
PostHog already syncs Stripe, HubSpot, Postgres, and the rest of your stack, so you can query it next to your events. The Warehouse goes one step further: it's an actual database, with its own hostname, credentials, and storage, holding your product data and business data side by side.
Point Metabase, a notebook, or a scheduled job at it, build your own tables in it, and run heavy queries without slowing down PostHog. It's the warehouse you'd otherwise set up in Snowflake or BigQuery, without the pipeline to get your product data into it.
PostHog’s AI features can use it as context, so asking "why did conversion drop?" comes back with the revenue impact, the cohorts affected, and what those users have in common.
