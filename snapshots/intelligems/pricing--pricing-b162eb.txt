FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How does the free trial work?
Every plan starts with a 14-day trial — no credit card required. You get full access to the entire Intelligems platform except Smart Pricing.
Try for free
What are the bundle discounts?
When you build your own plan you get 5% off with 2 modules, 10% off with 3, and 15% off with 4 or more. (Expert Support is excluded from the discount.)
What's the difference between Limited and Unlimited pricing?
Limited (Tier 1) includes 1 price test per month (12 per year) with a 3-month minimum commitment. Unlimited (Tier 2) gives you unlimited concurrent tests plus multi-currency pricing support, also with a 3-month minimum.
What's included in the Unlimited plan?
The Unlimited plan is Intelligems' all-in-one bundle that includes every module — Smart Content, Smart Pricing (with unlimited price tests and multi-currency support), Smart Checkout & Post-purchase, Smart Merchandising, and Smart Shipping — plus a Dedicated Customer Success Manager, storefront audit and testing roadmap, white glove onboarding and price integration, quarterly impact reviews, unlimited AI usage, custom integrations, and early access to new AI features.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can add or remove modules, switch tiers, or upgrade to Unlimited at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
