See what Kameleoon can do for you
Ask AI to identify ideas worth testing
Kameleoon AI scans your page for friction to build prioritized, prompt-ready experiments. It uses Predictive Impact Scoring, built on data from thousands of experiments, to ensure you launch ideas backed by real data, not intuition.
Build variants in minutes
Our agent generates test-ready variants, both front and back end. No manual coding required, but you can access, edit, or hand off the code at any time.
Target the right users, set the right goals
AI helps you target the right audience, assigns traffic, and picks the best metrics based on your goals.It adapts to visitor intent in real time to show the most relevant experience.
Learn what works and what to do next
Once the test runs, the agent analyzes results and explains what worked, what didn’t, and why. Get clear next steps and optimization ideas to increase impact.
