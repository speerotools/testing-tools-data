PROMPT BASED EXPERIMENTATION (PBX)
Full-cycle agentic experimentation
PBX is a connected system of agents unifying design, insights, execution, and performance on a continuous loop.
Agentic workflows:
Create, configure, and QA multiple experiments, managed through a unified Kanban workspace where every idea moves forward in parallel.
QA Agent:
An autonomous agent detects failures and routes them back for automated repair.
Full-stack Agent:
Extend AI-driven experimentation beyond the front-end; create backend services and admin interfaces.
PBX to Figma:
PBX generates Figma files from winning variants for engineering reuse.
INSIGHTS AND ANALYTICS
Advanced insights to scale your program
PBX Analyze clarifies how experiments impact key business metrics, surfacing essential learnings to help teams prioritize what to focus on next.
Built-in AI Assistant.
Query results and build reports you can pin and reuse.
Centralized idea engine.
Capture learnings and generate new ideas from past experiments to build a compounding knowledge base.
Goal tracking.
A single source of truth for reporting on goal performance and overall experimentation impact.
Ease of Use
Enabling experimentation at scale
We’re rethinking the foundations of the platform to make it smarter, safer, and better connected, so teams can experiment faster with full confidence.
Experiment rollbacks.
Ship with confidence and automatically roll back experiments when performance thresholds are crossed.
A/B test Personalization campaigns.
Fine-tune your personalization strategy with built-in A/B testing for personalization campaigns.
Multiple organizations per user.
Switch between multiple organizations without managing multiple sets of credentials.
